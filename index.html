  • Guides · Grok BotEmpty runs found while wiring 7 Grok Bot bots, and 5 fixesWiring 7 Grok Bot bots into a launch team, the author found hourly clocks mostly come back empty (1,176 runs a week by their math), email hand-offs pile up drafts awaiting approval, a test run does real work, and one bot can eat the shared X budget of 1,000 reads a day and 30 a minute. Their fixes: keep one clock (Monday 09:00) and make the rest triggers that fire when the previous bot writes DONE, hand off through a shared LEDGER file, give each bot its own X read cap, and let only one bot touch money with a $40 cap and approval on every buy.gippp692026-10-29
  • Guides · Grok BotFull Grok Bot launch-team prompt: 7 bots, 1 clockThe author shares the full prompt to paste into Grok Bot: work only in /workspace/launch/ with one folder per idea, hand off through files, have every bot read the LEDGER first and start only when the previous step is DONE, log every X search within 1,000 reads a day and 30 a minute, link facts and mark guesses GUESS, and never post, send, buy, pay or delete without approval. The 7 bots are Chief, Scout (the only clock, Monday 09:00), Pain Miner, Judge (scores 0 to 8 as GO, HOLD or KILL), Builder (one pull request, the author merges), Pitch (drafts only) and Buyer ($40 per idea, $100 a month, each purchase approved).gippp692026-10-29
