Scheduled routines, email/webhook, chat, Bot-to-Bot messages
Browser or connector
Built-in cloud browser + many connectors (Gmail, Calendar, Notion, GitHub…); browser steps cost the most
Parallelism
Many Bots in parallel, can spawn sub-agents; parallel count is the main quota multiplier
Routine frequency
Minutes to daily; frequent polling burns quota fastest
Good for

Not for
