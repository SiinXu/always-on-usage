Always-on Usage is a usage and workflow encyclopedia for always-on agents (Grok Bot, OpenAI Dots, Meta Muse), for people who want to run bots long-term without burning their quota: 1819 public entries and 26 X case studies so far, each with its author and source link.

We only do the evaluation layer

botsdots and grokbotwiki already do template directories well. Always-on Usage answers three questions: what each agent is good for, what burns quota, and how to build cheaply.

What we do
Usage maps, workflow comparisons, quota risk estimates, aggregated public cases (1819, all linked to sources). Most entries are Grok Bot; Dots and Muse public cases are added in incremental rounds
What we don't do
Template collecting or prompt packs. Use existing directories for templates.
About the numbers
Comparisons and the calculator are directional estimates, not vendor SLAs; case items only summarize public sources and never change numbers.
Languages
The interface and our own copy come in 10 languages; third-party titles and summaries stay in the original.
