Jev Router: a cheap decision layer in front of Grok Bot

Bodila51GitHub ↗Posted 2026-09-19Added 2026-10-15 13:40 UTC★ 108

What it does
Connects TypeSafe Jev to Grok Bot as a cheap decision layer that classifies requests before expensive research, browser, retry or subagent work, so the bot can reuse a fresh artifact, stop a failing retry, cap research or ask for approval. Includes usage gates, a skill template and examples.
