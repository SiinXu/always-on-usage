# 创意策略师把 Grok Bot 当团队成员来培训

Source: https://x.com/MathisMigue/status/2125937468067189025

Author: MathisMigue

What it does
The author says Grok Bot saves him 1–2 hours a day. His agents do brand research, find gigs, handle outreach and put together offers, plus editor briefs, early ad concepts, images and transcripts. He names each one, gives it a role, examples, training videos and feedback, and even interviews them. Some now write usable ad copy, but he keeps making the calls and automates bit by bit.
