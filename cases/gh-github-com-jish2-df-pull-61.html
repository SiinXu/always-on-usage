Letting Meta Muse and Grok Bot use a home Mac mini over MCP, safely

Jish2GitHub ↗Posted 2026-11-09Added 2026-11-09 23:43 UTC

What it does
The author wanted cloud agents (Meta Muse, Grok Bot) to reach his Mac mini over MCP for reminders, iMessage and files without exposing a shell to the internet. Desktop Commander runs behind a small local proxy published through a Cloudflare tunnel; the proxy re-verifies the Cloudflare Access JWT, exposes an allowlist of 17 tools, hides tools such as the config setter an agent could use to lift its own limits, and logs each call. In tests, missing, wrong-audience and expired JWTs got 401.
