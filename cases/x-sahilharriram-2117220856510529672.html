What it does
The author used Dots to plan a fairly complex Tasmania trip: a road trip and camping with his wife first, then meeting family who fly in later. He found its recommendations didn't consider practicalities, used it only as a starting point and built a better structure himself. He thinks experienced travellers can't rely 100% on AI for trip planning yet.
