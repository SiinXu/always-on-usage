Heads-up: Meta Muse can't shop on Amazon

@yasutaketinX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 22:52 UTC♥ 6

What it does
The author explains that since Muse launched in mid-September 2026, Amazon has blocked Muse from its site, showing a popup that continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates its Conditions of Use. Amazon says Meta didn't ask permission, the agent doesn't identify itself, and handling login details is risky. Alternatives are sites that aren't blocked, such as Walmart, Best Buy, Sephora, Wayfair and Shopify stores.
