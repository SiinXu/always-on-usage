Use plus aliases on your Grok Bot's email

@EverydayEddie_X ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 23:47 UTC

What it does
Tip from the author: if your Grok Bot has an email address, plus aliases like yourname+receipts land in the same inbox, and a routine watching that alias only runs for mail sent to it. Give each service its own alias, such as +receipts or +newsletters; note that anyone can email an alias.
