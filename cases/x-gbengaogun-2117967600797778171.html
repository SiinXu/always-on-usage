Each Dots task gets its own thread, continued in the tracker or the dot chat

@gbengaogunX ↗Posted 2026-11-04Added 2026-11-04 23:42 UTC

What it does
In part 4 of a thread, the author says each task has its own threaded conversation. He can comment on the work in the tracker, and the conversation can also continue in his dot chat, which keeps the context together and makes it easier to pick up where they left off.
