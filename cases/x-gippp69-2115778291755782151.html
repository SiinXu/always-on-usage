Empty runs found while wiring 7 Grok Bot bots, and 5 fixes

@gippp69X ↗Posted 2026-10-29Added 2026-10-29 12:46 UTC♥ 26

What it does
Wiring 7 Grok Bot bots into a launch team, the author found hourly clocks mostly come back empty (1,176 runs a week by their math), email hand-offs pile up drafts awaiting approval, a test run does real work, and one bot can eat the shared X budget of 1,000 reads a day and 30 a minute. Their fixes: keep one clock (Monday 09:00) and make the rest triggers that fire when the previous bot writes DONE, hand off through a shared LEDGER file, give each bot its own X read cap, and let only one bot touch money with a $40 cap and approval on every buy.

Risks and limits

  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
