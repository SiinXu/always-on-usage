What it does
The author grew a single bot into a 4-bot Grok Bot team focused on solo devs and small studios: Indie Scout searches X for new indie games and reports studio size, development stage and release date; Indie Spotlight Writer turns finds into spotlight posts, weekly wishlist threads and quote-post hooks; Indie Video Scripter writes 30 to 60 second vertical scripts and can make the video, thumbnail and images on request; Indie Digest Curator rolls the week into a newsletter or blog roundup with outreach questions for devs. Each bot is linked.
