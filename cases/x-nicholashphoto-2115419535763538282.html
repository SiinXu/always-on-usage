What it does
A solo photographer runs 17 bots on Grok Bot: a chief of staff that routes every job, plus bots for bookings, gallery pitches, an Etsy shop, marketing and SEO, bookkeeping and fact-checking, for about $200 a month. This week they fixed an Etsy listing, rescued 5 failed YouTube Shorts, wrote and voiced 2 videos and answered a gallery inquiry. Rule: nothing is sent, posted, bought or deleted without his OK.
