Steps to install and start using Grok Bot on Windows

@LionieioX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 22:52 UTC♥ 24

What it does
A video guide: open the download page, click Download for Windows, run the installer and pick who to install for and a folder; open Grok Bot and connect your X account; in Settings under Usage & Billing click Manage Plan, confirm the Free plan and add a Visa or Mastercard for billing; finally sign in on the web with X and authorize the app.
