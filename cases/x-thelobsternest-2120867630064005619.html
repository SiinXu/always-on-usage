Your first AI bot shouldn't be allowed to press any buttons

@TheLobsterNestX ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 13:41 UTC

What it does
The author notes the official Grok Bot starter prompts end with "don't send anything" and "don't book anything". For small business owners: pick one weekly chore; ask like you'd ask a coworker (output, sources, limits, when to check in); let it gather and draft while you press the final button; keep passwords and cards out of chat; schedule it only once results are right every time.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
