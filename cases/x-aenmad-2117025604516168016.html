Day 2 with Grok Bot: selling his stuff, booking doctors, building a moving checklist

@AENMADX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 23:42 UTC♥ 1

What it does
Moving abroad, the author gave Grok Bot (he calls it Walter) his Facebook Marketplace and Gmail. It grouped and priced his items, wrote and posted the ads, and checks buyer messages every 30 minutes to answer simple questions; emails go out only after he reviews them. In two days they sold two big items, filed warranty claims, booked two doctor appointments plus bloodwork, found savings in bank and card fees, and built a moving checklist. He paid a $29 first-month promo price.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Uses a browser or website login: the costliest kind of step, so prefer a connector where one exists
