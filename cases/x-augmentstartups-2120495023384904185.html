Five Grok Bots to start with in a small business

@AugmentStartupsX ↗Posted 2026-11-11Added 2026-11-11 23:43 UTC♥ 2

What it does
The author suggests not asking one bot to run everything but giving each a clear job: Sales (research prospects and a shortlist with reasons), Content (notes into draft posts to check), Research (compare suppliers, show sources and gaps), Admin (inbox and calendar in one short brief) and Ops (clean a copy of a messy spreadsheet and flag issues). Start with the job you keep putting off and check the work before giving more responsibility.
