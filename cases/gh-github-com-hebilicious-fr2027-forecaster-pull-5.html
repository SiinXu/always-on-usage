Grok Bot blind-checks polls and edits the candidate list, no human in the loop

HebiliciousGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-16Added 2026-10-16 11:12 UTC

What it does
A France 2027 election forecaster makes Grok Bot the only agent in its pipeline. A new routine re-enters each proposed poll from the original notice without seeing the proposal's numbers; only an exact match is committed to main, a mismatch is rejected without revealing values. Structured candidate edits are validated and logged. Runs at 10:30 and 20:30 Paris time.
