# 用 Grok Bot 翻 Amazon 旧订单找回退款：四个实例

Author: p_kuperman

What it does
A roundup: @cstanley had Grok Bot check all his Amazon orders and it found $590.62 in missing refunds, then got support to agree to issue them; @trevin uploaded his order-history export and it found $892 owed since 2017 under a class action (the email was in spam); @leerob had it dig through email and file a form over a $100 wrong charge; @Michael_Fenech_ splits money access into see, prepare and approve, with every payment needing his yes.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
