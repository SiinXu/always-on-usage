Steps / points from the source
  1. ✅ Read a message you paste (or a screenshot you attach) and give a clear verdict
  2. ✅ Quote the exact red flags and explain the trick simply
  3. ✅ Make a shareable Report Card for warning friends and family
  4. ✅ Prepare a ready-to-submit evidence pack for 7726 / FTC reports
  5. ❌ Have a phone number — you can't forward texts to it; you paste or attach the message in the chat
  6. ❌ File reports or contact anyone on its own — every external action needs your YES, every time
  7. ❌ Ask for passwords, bank logins, or your SSN — ever
  8. ❌ See your other messages, contacts, or photos
