Using Muse for large-scale web scraping for a concert-video site

Scott CooperHacker News · sigh.dev ↗Posted 2026-10-01Added 2026-10-15 03:19 UTC▲ 63

What it does
For fullsets.fm, which collects full professionally recorded concerts, the author has Muse scan Reddit and YouTube for videos and obscure channels, and review artist matches at the end of the pipeline. $80 for a month, a few billion tokens a week; the model is mediocre at writing and not for coding but good for this. Jobs sometimes freeze, and he expects more sites to block Muse.
