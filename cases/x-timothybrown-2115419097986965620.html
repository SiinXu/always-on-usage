Read-only remote access to a Mac’s Mail, Calendar and Messages for Grok Bot

@TimothyBrownX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 12:37 UTC

What it does
The author lets their Grok Bot assistant use a Mac mini’s Mail, Messages, Calendar, Contacts and Maps read-only over MCP. The chain: Grok Bot connector (HTTPS + Bearer token) to Tailscale Funnel to Caddy checking the token to supergateway (stdio to HTTP) to iMCP. Without a valid token you get a 401. The assistant flags conflicts between work and personal calendars in the morning rundown.
