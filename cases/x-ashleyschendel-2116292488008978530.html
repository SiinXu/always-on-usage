Parent Command Center: sorting the family schedule from school emails

@ashleyschendelX ↗Posted 2026-10-30Added 2026-10-30 22:41 UTC♥ 2

What it does
The author built a Grok Bot called Parent Command Center. It pulls dates, deadlines and to-dos from school emails and activity schedules, checks for conflicts, and prepares calendar updates for her to approve. She still decides what gets added or changed, but no longer digs through every message for details. She shares a link to the bot.
