What it does
The workflow from the author's video: one 41-minute talk and 12 saved links in; 5 posts, 1 newsletter and 3 vertical clips out for X, YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, with 0 auto posts. Rules: one bot, one job; do the task together, save it as a skill, press Test, then schedule (up to 50 routines per Bot, at least 5 minutes apart); draft everything, with approvals coming straight to you; pick the slowest schedule that works. Each bot gets a 4-line first message: Job, Sources, Approval line, Remember. The post also lists open-source repos that fit the line.

Risks and limits
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Uses a browser or website login: the costliest kind of step, so prefer a connector where one exists
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
