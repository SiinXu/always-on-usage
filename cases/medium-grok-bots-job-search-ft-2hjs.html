Three cooperating Grok Bots plus a manager bot to find referrers

Roshan AmbhoreMedium ↗Posted 2026-10-12Added 2026-10-15 13:40 UTC

What it does
Following the 2HJS method, the author built three bots that hand off in order — FPB finds potential boosters, Hunter finds emails, Kaka drafts the email — and added a Manager bot when Hunter got stuck, to replace the contact and send FPB looking again. He still checks LinkedIn and sends emails himself.
