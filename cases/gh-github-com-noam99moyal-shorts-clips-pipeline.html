Steps / points from the source
  1. Open the Grok Bot template: Shorts Clips Pipeline
  2. Open the new agent chat and finish first-run setup. The bot asks these once and saves your answers.
  3. A Google Drive folder for cut lists, copy docs, clips, and the schedule log (confirmed with a path and link)
  4. A voice or style file for titles and descriptions, or the default voice
  5. Caption rules (defaults shown; change anything you want)
  6. YouTube Studio sign-in in the bot's own browser, through a screen handoff. Never send a password in chat.
  7. You share a transcript (or the bot makes one with ffmpeg audio extract and faster-whisper) and the source video, plus what you want: topic, speaker, how many……
  8. It picks self-contained 40-60s clips (70s hard max) that open on a full, no-context sentence, make one point, and end on a strong line.
