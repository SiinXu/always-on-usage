Ten steps to stop running out of Grok Bot usage: from Thursday to 54%

@0xRafyX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 23:41 UTC♥ 4

What it does
The author says 43% of the weekly limit goes to waste: routines waking to empty inboxes and bots rereading old chats. Steps include auditing every routine; cutting a 15-minute routine (672 runs a week) to at most hourly on weekday work hours; swapping polling for Slack, GitHub, email or webhook triggers; restarting long threads with a handoff note; one job per bot; a finish line and stop after two failures; and a Wednesday check under 43% with low-usage mode at 80%. The example setup ran out on Thursday and ended the week at 54% after the changes.
