Wired's six-agent bake-off: Dots wins, at $100 a month

What it does
Per the post, a Wired reporter ran Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, Instinct, Claude Duet, Gemini Spark and Grok Bot through five tasks. Dots scored 5/5 on lunch and the calendar-email clash, Muse won the China itinerary, Gemini Spark wrote the best headline but scored 1/5 on lunch, and Instinct was most annoying. Prices: Dots $100, Muse free and a close second, Claude Duet a $20 preview, Grok Bot $30, Gemini Spark $20.
