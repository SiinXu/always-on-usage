Manage your Dot like an employee

@tomcohenX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 12:37 UTC

What it does
Tracking everything in the single Dots chat didn’t work, so the author treats his Dot, Elminster, like an engineer he manages: Elminster documents project status, next steps, blockers and decisions needed in a Notion workspace, and they hold daily 1:1s. He says it’s far from perfect but works much better than chat alone.
