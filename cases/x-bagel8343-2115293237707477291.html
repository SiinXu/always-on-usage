What it does
Based on official material as of 2026-10-28, the author suggests a trial order: start with Meta Muse for personal preferences and life admin, and evaluate Grok Bot first for recurring research, file delivery and explicit responsibilities. Run about ten real tasks and track acceptable deliverables, review minutes, serious errors, recovery and total cost. He stresses it is not a tested ranking.
