Triggering a Grok Bot routine from iPhone Shortcuts

@Nicolo_TognoniX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 23:41 UTC

What it does
An iPhone Shortcuts automation starts a Grok Bot routine when an iMessage with a keyword arrives from one sender, and passes the message text in. No app to open: the routine only needs a webhook trigger, and the same trick works for any app that can send a POST request.
