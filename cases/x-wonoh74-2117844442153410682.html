Meta Muse workflow: build a monthly expense dashboard, then add rows by asking

@wonoh74X ↗Posted 2026-11-04Added 2026-11-04 13:37 UTC

What it does
The author’s Meta Muse workflow: ask it to “make a dashboard tracking my monthly expenses” and Muse builds it and saves it to your Library. Later just say “add a row for March”; Muse remembers files from the conversation, so no re-uploading. Also works with XLSX, CSV, PDF and DOCX.
