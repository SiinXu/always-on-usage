grokbot-home — move a whole Grok Bot fleet to a new account

RongleCat/awesome-grok-bot (GitHub) ↗

Title and summary are in the original language, not translated.

What it does
One user's public "home" repo for switching Grok Bot accounts: a Bot on the new account reads BOOTSTRAP.md, asks for a passphrase to decrypt the Composio key, installs tools, then recreates every other Bot from saved profiles, memories and routines with CreateAgent and writes what it learned back.
