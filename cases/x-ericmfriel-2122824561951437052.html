# 省 Grok Bot 用量的三条建议

Source: https://x.com/EricMFriel/status/2122824561951437052

Author: EricMFriel

作者给出三条建议：让机器人自己清理过时的例行任务；尽量用应用连接器，减少机器人在浏览器里的时间，因为这很耗用量；与 Grok Bot 例行任务无关的事改用 Meta Muse，分摊用量成本。

What it does
The author's three tips: ask your bot to optimize by removing obsolete routines; use app connectors as much as possible to limit browser time, which is costly in usage; and use Meta Muse for things unrelated to Grok Bot routines to spread the usage cost.
