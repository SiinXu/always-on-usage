A bot that audits the other bots cut his usage about 5x in a week

@Simonpuebla_X ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 23:42 UTC

What it does
His quota was running out in 3 days, so the author built Lean, a bot that audits his other bots. The biggest win was not dragging huge context along: work 5 things at a time and save long results to a file instead of pasting them in chat; fix 3 corrections in one go; start a new chat each week with a short summary of what's pending. A week later usage was about 5x lower, and his sales bot dropped from about 20% of consumption to about 3% while working even better. He includes the prompt for building the audit agent.
