Steps / points from the source
  1. Open a fresh Grokbot.
  2. Paste the prompt from BOOTSTRAP-PROMPT.md with this repo's link, or attach dist/content-grokbot-kit-v1.0.0.zip.
  3. Answer the setup interview (about 45–60 minutes, no homework).
  4. docs/USER-GUIDE.md: how to use it, day to day.
  5. kit/INSTALL.md: what the Grokbot does during install.
  6. docs/KIT-SPEC.md: every rule, format, and decision behind the kit.
  7. Edit docs/KIT-SPEC.md first.
  8. Edit the kit files to match.
