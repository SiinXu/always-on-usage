# 一家施工企业本月用 AI 做的自动化清单

Source: https://x.com/bprintco/status/2122314734200344838

Author: bprintco

作者列出本月用 AI 做成的事：淡季线索量涨 4 倍，自动化 OSHA 安全培训与合规跟踪，做了月度邮件通讯，操作员手机离线查看工单范围和进度，复杂报价从 1 小时缩到 5 分钟，自动统计每小时清理速度和收入，每天扫描数百个商业和政府标书并自动生成投标包。作者认为只用 Grok Bot 只能发挥 10%，要结合硬件、按任务选模型。

What it does
The author lists this month's results with AI: 4x lead volume in a usually slow season, automated OSHA safety training and compliance tracking, a monthly email newsletter, offline job scopes on operator phones, complex estimates cut from one hour to 5 minutes, automatic clearing-speed and revenue-per-hour metrics, and a bid tool that scans hundreds of commercial and government bids daily and generates bid packets. They say Grok Bot alone gets only 10% of what's possible without mixing hardware and picking models per task.
