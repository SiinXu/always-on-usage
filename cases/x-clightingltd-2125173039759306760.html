# iPhone 上 Grok Bot 例行任务的限制

Source: https://x.com/clightingltd/status/2125173039759306760

Author: clightingltd

作者反馈：在 iPhone 上，日历、提醒事项、通讯录和定位只在 Grok Bot 应用打开时可用，所以例行任务无法在后台调用它们；若能后台访问，手机会像已上线的 Mac 应用一样有用。

What it does
The author reports that on iPhone, Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and Location only work while the Grok Bot app is open, so a routine can't use them in the background; background access would make the phone as useful as the Mac apps already shipped.
