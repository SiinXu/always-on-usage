A playlist-generator app built inside Meta Muse

@vibecodeguildX ↗Posted 2026-10-16Added 2026-10-16 12:43 UTC♥ 2

What it does
The author used Meta Muse's built-in Spotify connector plus a custom connector for a third-party music-data site, since Spotify's API doesn't expose its recommendation engine, to build an app that generates playlists. Muse set up a routine that crawls songs to find related tracks, after some corrections, and gave the app a 70s funky disco look. The author says it was free to build and needed no hosting or security setup.
