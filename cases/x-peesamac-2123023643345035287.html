Grok Bot on Mac now connects to 7 built-in Apple apps

@peesamacX ↗Posted 2026-11-18Added 2026-11-18 23:46 UTC♥ 1

What it does
Per the Grok Bot account's 13 Nov announcement, Grok Bot on Mac can connect to 7 Apple apps: Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location and Contacts. A settings page called Connect Apps lists them to Allow one by one and explains what each permission lets it do.
