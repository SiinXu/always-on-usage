What it does
The post describes how @paolo_scales turned the research, evidence and review that LinkedIn posts usually skip into 12 Grok Bot prompts: load your buyers, positioning, approved proof and real voice; mine sales calls, closed-lost notes and X for questions; turn transcripts into a 4-week calendar and drafts with one argument; score drafts against a 100-point rubric and check every claim; connect posts to stalled deals and tracked results; and feed your edits back into the voice guide. Grok Bot prepares research, drafts and reports, while you keep the opinion, evidence and every publish decision.
