What it does
The post compares the two. Grok Bot (xAI) is a multi-agent system: several named teammates share one cloud computer, work in parallel across apps and sites 24/7, hand off tasks and join Slack/Teams, from $30/mo Premium. A ChatGPT Dot is OpenAI's single always-on personal agent with its own cloud computer, learning your preferences and connecting to 4000+ apps, included with Pro ($100+). Both keep working while your device is off.
