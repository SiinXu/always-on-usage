A single dad spends an hour each Sunday reviewing his week with Grok Bot

@TonyChanBuildsX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 23:42 UTC♥ 4

What it does
The author runs a business, applies for 9-to-5 roles, creates content and raises two kids. Every Sunday he spends an hour talking with Grok Bot about how he spent his time, his wins, the challenges that came up and the next steps. Grok Bot then fills in his calendar for the week based on that conversation.
