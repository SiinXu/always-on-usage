A shared Notion context layer so every teammate's agent reads the same facts

@temncoX ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC

What it does
From the author's Claude Community House talk: each person's context lives in their own AI chat, so people end up copy-pasting between agents. His fix is a shared context layer in Notion holding tasks, docs, meetings, decisions, projects and skills, read by every teammate's agent, whether Claude, Grok Bot or a custom harness. The video covers the architecture and demos of tasks, meetings and decisions.
