Building Skylark Lake in Unreal Engine with a dot

@DylanBishopXX ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 23:47 UTC♥ 2

What it does
With his dot in ChatGPT, using Fable 5.2 to plan and Opus 5.5 to build, the author made Skylark Lake in Unreal Engine: click to pull them toward the cursor, right-click to scatter them. A first try with 6.1 Sol wasn't great, which he puts down to his own context prompting; on the second run the dot supplied more of his context. A web-game version was too much of a GPU hog.
