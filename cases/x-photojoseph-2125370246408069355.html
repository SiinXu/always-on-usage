# Grok Bot 做出可操控的 3D 缩略图模型

Source: https://x.com/photojoseph/status/2125370246408069355

Author: photojoseph

作者让 Grok Bot 为一条 Neewer 视频做缩略图构思，又问它能否做个能在 Grok 应用里操控的 3D 模型。结果它做到了：可切换环绕与缩略图机位、调焦距、有预设和俯视图，支持捏合拖动和滑块，还能开关缩略图文字。作者附了 v1 链接。

What it does
The author had a Grok Bot work on thumbnail concepts for a Neewer video, then asked if it could build a 3D model to manipulate in the Grok app. It did: it toggles orbit and thumbnail camera views, changes lens focal lengths, has presets and a top-down view, works with pinch, drag and sliders, and toggles the thumbnail text. He shared the v1 link.
