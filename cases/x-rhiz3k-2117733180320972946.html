40,618 usage records: how many tokens Grok Bot gives per month

@Rhiz3KX ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC

What it does
From 40,618 Grok Bot usage records (Oct 13 to Nov 3) the author estimates about 1.97B tokens a month on Premium (€30) and 7.35B on Super (€100), plus a cloud computer his shell reported as 8 vCPU / 15 GiB / 128 GB. He compares this with Claude and ChatGPT plans, EU VPS hosting and managed bot hosts. Caveats: the Super figure is extrapolated from 4.64 days and 93.6% of its tokens were cached reads.
