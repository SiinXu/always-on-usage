Grok Bot drops connector calls after two minutes, so cap waits at 100 s

foxkillGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-22Added 2026-10-22 22:41 UTC

What it does
A test in Grok Bot showed it calls get_session_updates with wait_seconds: 120, while Grok Bot gives up on a connector call after two minutes without progress. Version 0.1.11 caps each wait at 100 s for hosts that do not ask for progress, returning status: running so the next call continues; hosts with a progressToken keep the full wait and get progress every 10 s.
