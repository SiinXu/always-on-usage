A Grok Bot schedule setup: the main bot runs only two routines

@TodayKanX ↗Posted 2026-10-27Added 2026-10-27 12:39 UTC

What it does
The author keeps using Grok Bot heavily because its cloud computer stays signed in and routines wake on their own within time windows. His main bot has just two: a daily update Monday to Saturday between 10 and 22, and a review at 21. Specialist bots act only when called, write conclusions to disk and read them first next round. Anything sent out needs his approval.
