Cutting Grok Bots from 20 back to 5: more is harder to manage

@rimulab9X ↗Posted 2026-11-04Added 2026-11-04 23:42 UTC

What it does
The author grew to 20 Grok Bots and then went back to 5. More bots meant more mental load and losing track of who was asked to do what; bots also started consulting each other and quietly burned tokens, until he couldn't tell which bot owned a task. With 5 it is easier to keep track, and he recommends it.
