Wary of Dots, Grok Bot and Muse, he built a local email assistant

@lesithX ↗Posted 2026-11-18Added 2026-11-18 23:46 UTC♥ 1

What it does
Over trust concerns the author built Dragonfly on a Mac mini: it watches chosen Gmail and Apple Mail folders on a schedule, remembers people and conversations in an Obsidian vault, drafts replies when someone is waiting, and pings him on Telegram. It sends only when he replies "send" to that exact draft (sending is plain code, not AI); emails are treated as information, never instructions; a gate checks every tool call; every change can be undone with one click. Built with Claude Code, Python and Swift.
