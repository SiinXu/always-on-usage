What it does
Based on reports so far: Duet has no separate price and runs on your Claude plan, Grok Bot starts at $30, Dots at $100, and Muse is free to start. Grok Bot runs several named agents while Duet is one thread; Dots can optionally use your laptop; Duet asks before it sends, pays, signs or deletes, and its background checks count toward Claude limits.
