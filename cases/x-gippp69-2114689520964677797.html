Rebuilding routines after doing the math on idle runs

@gippp69X ↗Posted 2026-10-26Added 2026-10-26 12:44 UTC♥ 41

What it does
Before moving his setup to Grok Bot, the author did the math: a routine every 5 minutes is 8,640 runs a month, hourly is 720, which he puts at $397 versus $33 for the same job, 12x. So he rebuilt his routines so that each one runs a check first.
