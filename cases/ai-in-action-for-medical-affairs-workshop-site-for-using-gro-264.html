Steps / points from the source
  1. Start in three steps. Copy a link, give it to your agent, then review what it makes.
  2. Missions. Ready-made workshop exercises (field insights, congress, launch planning and more) that use fictional, clearly labelled synthetic data.
  3. Prompt Optimizer. Type a short goal such as *"Build a launch plan for ADIPOSYN"* and get back a full, goal-oriented assignment for Grok Bot. It can also set u…
  4. Get the data. Two big buttons: *Download all synthetic data (.zip)* and *Download full catalog (.json/.csv)*. Below them is a searchable list of every dataset…
  5. Grok Bot install. Step-by-step setup. Sign-up links and credit codes are *shared at the conference*.
  6. Agent-ready. AI agents can read agents.md, llms.txt and the .json files to do everything the skills library supports.
  7. Choose Single task for Grok Bot or Agent swarm setup.
  8. Type a short goal, or tap one of the starters.
