# Grok Bot 套餐用量多约 11%，例程最高多 50%

Source: https://x.com/ExactSolid/status/2126088148660965526

Author: ExactSolid

据 Grok Bot 团队的 Sam Sokolin，套餐现在可多用约 11%，例程最高可多 50%。原因是例程运行框架会对简单、重复的任务跳过模型推理：成本下降来自少调用模型，而不只是更便宜的 token。

What it does
Per Sam Sokolin of the Grok Bot team, plans now stretch about 11% further and routines up to 50%. The routine harness skips model inference for simple, repetitive tasks, so agents get cheaper by calling the model less, not just through cheaper tokens.
