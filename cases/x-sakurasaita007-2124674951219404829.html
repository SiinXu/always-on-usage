Hands-on review: Meta Muse for AI filmmaking

@Sakura_saita007X ↗Posted 2026-11-23Added 2026-11-23 13:39 UTC♥ 2

What it does
Testing Muse for the AI film project WORLD is YOURS: image generation is excellent; video motion is natural but character identity is unstable, and Grok Imagine held identity better on the same references; external photorealistic character images can trigger video restrictions; there are no keyframe or timestamp controls, and characters sometimes spoke when told to stay silent. Not yet suitable as a primary tool; only free video generation was tested.
