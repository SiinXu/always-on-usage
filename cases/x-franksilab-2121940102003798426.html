Grok Bot caught a test the school slipped in on a Friday night

@Frank_SI_LabX ↗Posted 2026-11-15Added 2026-11-15 13:38 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author's setup: a school bot checks the timetable every morning, read-only; when a new test appears, a calendar bot adds it after a duplicate check; a chief-of-staff bot keeps everyone in sync; once he passes on what the test covers, the kids' English, math and German coach bots turn it into custom practice tests. He says the bot, not the parents or the group chat, caught next week's test.
