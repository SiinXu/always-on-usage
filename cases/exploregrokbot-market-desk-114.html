Steps / points from the source
  1. Keep company/mission.md, company/opportunity-criteria.md, and company/owner-context.md current. Agents may use only documented Owner Fit facts; undocument
  2. Point an agent at agents/market-research-analyst/ and have it follow instructions.md.
  3. File Researcher1 / Market Research Analyst assignments under research/assignments/fromResearcher1/. Researcher2 uses research/assignments/fromResearcher2/ (
  4. File other finished write-ups under research/reports/ when they are not numbered assignments.
  5. File scored opportunities under research/opportunities/.
  6. Record go / no-go / defer choices in decisions/decision-log.md.
  7. Capture reusable mistakes and wins in agents/market-research-analyst/lessons-learned.md.
  8. Assignments (Researcher1): research/assignments/fromResearcher1/NNN-.md (example: 001-ttrpg-problem-discovery.md)
