Rinde: snap a grocery list, compare live prices in Colombia

@TinHoChuX ↗Posted 2026-10-30Added 2026-10-30 12:46 UTC♥ 12

What it does
For the Meta hackathon the author built Rinde with Meta Muse: snap your shopping list and it checks live prices at D1, Jumbo, Éxito and Carulla, then tells you where to buy what and how much you save. The starting point: Colombian families overpay for groceries every week.
