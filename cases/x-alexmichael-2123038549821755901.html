Grok Bot tip: a webhook instead of checking news every 5 minutes

@alexmichaelX ↗Posted 2026-11-18Added 2026-11-18 13:45 UTC♥ 7

What it does
The author's second webhook tip: don't have your bot check the news every 5 minutes — that is 288 runs a day even when nothing is new. Point an RSS watcher or a tiny script at your feeds; when a new story drops it POSTs the title and link to your routine, so the bot only wakes up when there is something new.
