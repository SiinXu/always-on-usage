Grok Bot as full-time travel assistant on a family road trip

@PipeSalazarX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 23:41 UTC

What it does
On a Tesla trip through Munich, Nancy, Reims, Disneyland Paris, Paris and Colmar, Grok Bot kept a live day-by-day page with Supercharger stops, sent the author’s wife lodging shortlists in Spanish, emailed a hotel in French to learn reception closed at 21:00, and booked and rescheduled a Reims lunch with a high chair. Grok in the car could reach the plan and called the night staff when they arrived late.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
