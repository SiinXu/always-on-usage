# 省额度：把例行任务改成本地脚本，只在需要时唤醒 Bot

Source: https://x.com/z80srl/status/2116145921520238674

Author: z80srl

作者说他们把大部分耗额度的例行任务改成了 Grok Bot 机器上的小型本地脚本。这些脚本不消耗额度，只在结果符合预设情况时才唤醒 Bot。例子：脚本监控银行流水，没有变动时 Bot 就继续休眠。

What it does
The author says they moved most quota-hungry routines into small local scripts on the Grok Bot machine. The scripts use no quota and only wake the bot when the result matches a planned case. Example: a script watches bank movements, and if nothing moved, the bot stays asleep.
