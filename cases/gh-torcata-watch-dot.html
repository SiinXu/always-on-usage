watch-dot: chat with your Dot on Apple Watch

TorcataGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-04Added 2026-10-15 13:40 UTC

What it does
An Apple Watch client to type or dictate to your Dot in the same conversation as ChatGPT; the iPhone is only needed to sign in, then the watch works over Wi-Fi or cellular, with a complication. It uses the desktop app’s protocol and may break if OpenAI changes it.

Steps / points from the source
  1. **Chat directly with your Dot:** type or dictate a message, tap the arrow, and the reply arrives on your watch. It's the same conversation you see in ChatGPT.
  2. **Use your Dot without your iPhone:** the iPhone is only needed to sign in. After that, the watch chats and renews the session on its own, over Wi‑Fi or cellula
  3. **On-demand sync:** to pull in messages sent from other devices, scroll to the bottom of the chat and swipe up.
  4. **Watch face complication:** a shortcut to open the chat with a single tap.
  5. **Push notifications**
  6. **Voice replies**
  7. Mac with Xcode and Swift 6
  8. iPhone running iOS 17 or later, with Developer Mode enabled
