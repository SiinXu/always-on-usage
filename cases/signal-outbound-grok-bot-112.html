Steps / points from the source
  1. Drop anyone with no why-now (no dated public signal on the record: an engagement, a post, a job change, a raise, or the equivalent field the backend actuall
  2. Sort what remains: icp_score descending, then title match against the business ICP, then geo match, then recency.
  3. Cap at 8.
  4. Max watches keep collecting on the API whether your laptop is open or not.
  5. This Grok Bot briefs on weekday mornings (or when you hunt). It is not a 24/7 sender and it does not blast overnight because a digest exists.
  6. A human sends in this recipe.
