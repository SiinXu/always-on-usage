$30 of inference: Grok Bot turns a floor plan and inspiration board into a renovation preview

@gkhalinX ↗Posted 2026-11-15Added 2026-11-15 23:46 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author wanted to see his apartment after renovation. Through Grok Bot he fed xAI Grok 4.7 his floor plan, first reconstructed a 3D model, then gave it the model plus a link to the home design inspiration board he had collected on Savee for months. After 5 iterations using voice input he was happy with the result. He says it took about $30 of inference; a professional would still have plenty to figure out, but he can now say what he wants with much less ambiguity.
