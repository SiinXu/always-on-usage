A practical setup guide for lead research with Grok Bot

@earifcomX ↗Posted 2026-10-16Added 2026-10-16 12:43 UTC

What it does
The author proposes an adaptable setup: write a one-page brief (offer, buyer, current signals, allowed sources, exclusions); create one "Prospect Research Desk" bot that must never guess contacts, send messages or modify the CRM; run a small pilot prompt (up to 5 businesses, at most 15 source pages); review, deduplicate and sort into three statuses; after two useful batches save it as a skill and schedule it on weekdays; only then add a controlled CRM handoff and follow-up. The author notes it is a blueprint, not a client case, and nothing was executed.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Uses a browser or website login: the costliest kind of step, so prefer a connector where one exists
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
