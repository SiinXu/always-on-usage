The 7 Grok Bots a marketing agency owner runs

Sharbel A.YouTube ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 23:47 UTCviews 3,106

What it does
The author runs a marketing agency and shows seven Grok Bots he uses to close deals, prep meetings, create content, find leads, protect client relationships and catch wasted spend. He says the Inbox Closer booked 18 calls and helped bring in an extra $10,000 in October, the Scavenger saved $5,000 on marketing costs, and the Money Finder found $2,800 a year in forgotten subscriptions and refundable charges. There is also a Meeting Briefer, Content Engine, Lead Machine, Client Success Manager and a Chief of Staff that combines all bots into one daily message; a human reviews anything important before it is sent or changed.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
