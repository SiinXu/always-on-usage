What it does
The author didn't want to start the day on her phone but needed to stay on top of work, so she built a 12-page morning digest that prints before she wakes. It is connected to her Grok Bot, email, calendar and what her AI knows about her interests, and pulls together work priorities and to-dos, important updates, news and picked articles.
