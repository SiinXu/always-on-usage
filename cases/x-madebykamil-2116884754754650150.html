Grok Bot drafts a skill from a recorded browser workflow

@madebykamilX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 13:46 UTC

What it does
When the feature is available, Grok Bot drafts a skill from a browser workflow you record, up to 10 minutes. The author suggests recording a weekly task such as pulling a dashboard report; review and save the draft, and your bots can reuse it once the login is connected.
