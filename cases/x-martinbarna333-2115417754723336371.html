Keep recurring routines off your main bot

@MartinBarna333X ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 12:37 UTC

What it does
The author set up one bot just for routines; it runs in a short clean chat, so the main chat doesn’t burn tokens. Routines run at most 1 to 2 times a day, preferably on an event such as an incoming email rather than a timer. Know-how lives in files, not in the bot, so a replacement bot picks up right away.
