What it does
The author lists what Grok Bot does for them: it spins up helper bots and delegates to them; typing 'make the button pink' gets handed to a Cursor agent and comes back as a ready pull request; it builds live dashboards and mini tools in the chat with no code from them; it joins Zoom or Google Meet calls and takes notes; it has its own email inbox and its own computer with a browser; and it makes videos and images itself.
