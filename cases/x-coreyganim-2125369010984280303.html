What it does
The author once onboarded OpenClaw agents with four files; now he spins up a Grok Bot in four seconds. His process: 1) profile with name, title, description and one key job; 2) shared memory with his name, time zone, offers and ICP; 3) a voice skill and behavior rules such as no em-dashes and draft only; 4) skills and routines, e.g. a Sunday 8am weekly plan in Todoist; 5) teammates that hand off work, like Concept, Craft and Analytics bots. When he edits a draft, Craft Bot records the lesson as a rule.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
