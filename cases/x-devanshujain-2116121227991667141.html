Dots escalates a stuck App Store review

@devanshujain_X ↗Posted 2026-10-30Added 2026-10-30 12:46 UTC♥ 2

What it does
The author's iOS app had sat in 'Waiting for review' for 20 days. Dots first checked whether anything was pending on his side, such as issues or agreements, then found the App Store support emails, wrote a message and sent it to the right address. About 20 hours later he got an email saying the app was in the expedited review queue.
