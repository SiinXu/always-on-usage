Ubuntu eCryptfs: sand-client ENAMETOOLONG blocks first setup

RongleCat/awesome-grok-bot (GitHub) ↗

Title and summary are in the original language, not translated.

What it does
Staff (Colin, 2026-09-13): Grok Bot stores ~140–200-char files under ~/.config/Grok Bot/sand-client-persistence/; Linux allows 255 but eCryptfs (“Encrypt my home folder”) caps names at 143 → ENAMETOOLONG on Ubuntu first setup. Check with getconf NAME_MAX ~/.config and mount | grep -i ecryptfs; team is shortening names.
