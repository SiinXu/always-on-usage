Nyuki Lens: a hive-health advisor for Kenyan beekeepers

ngetichkipkiruiGitHub ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC

What it does
The open-source Nyuki Lens is a FastAPI hive-health advisor: upload a photo of a frame, apiary, bee or pest and get a short diagnosis and one action, in English or Swahili. It is powered by Meta SAM 3.1 and Muse Spark 1.1.
