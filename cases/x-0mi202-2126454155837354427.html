# 让 Grok Bot 替我打客服电话

Source: https://x.com/0mi202/status/2126454155837354427

Author: 0mi202

作者说现在由 Grok Bot 替他拨打客服电话，结果是 AI 对 AI 走一遍电话菜单。他认为问题在于电话流程本身，设想企业把客服系统直接接入 Grok Bot：说一句“账单错了，帮我改”，Bot 在企业真实政策内处理并给出结果。

What it does
The author says Grok Bot now makes customer support calls for him, so it is AI talking to AI through a phone tree. He argues the phone flow is the problem and imagines companies connecting support directly to Grok Bot: say "my bill is wrong, fix it" and the bot handles it within the company's real policies.
