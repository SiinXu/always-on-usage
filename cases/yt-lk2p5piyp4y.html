What it does
The author describes Jim, who has severe neuropathy and runs most of his life by voice through a team of GrokBot bots: one tracks his medication, one looks after his car, and one calls the hotel before travel to check the room is accessible. The video covers 8 ways people with disabilities use GrokBot and links templates such as a Deaf Assistant, Dad Bot and an ADHD bot.
