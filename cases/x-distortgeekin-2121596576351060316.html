What it does
Following SpaceXAI's Lauren Tan, the author let one Grok Bot hire the others for their Gmail support inbox: 3 bots in 4 minutes, one job each. Sorter reads only the subject and first 3 lines; Researcher searches the help center and brings back 3 articles max. Video attached.
