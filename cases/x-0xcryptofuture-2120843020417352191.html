When Grok Bot runs out of usage, rank your bots by what a pause costs

@0x_CryptoFutureX ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-13 13:39 UTC♥ 10

What it does
The author warns that a bot handling leads, a support inbox or invoices can hit its weekly limit while a client waits. The article he cites sorts bots by what happens when they pause: MONEY (clients, support, invoices, revenue), TIME (research, reports, drafts) and NICE (dashboards, extra briefings). His setup: know which bots matter most, keep a human fallback for client-facing work, and leave enough weekly allowance for the jobs that keep the business running, before buying another top-up.
