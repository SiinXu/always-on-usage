One person runs 7 YouTube channels with a Grok Bot team

@ashnichristX ↗Posted 2026-10-20Added 2026-10-20 22:41 UTC♥ 9

What it does
Alongside a full-time job, the author uses a team of bots to manage 7 YouTube channels and post 21 talking-head clips, 14 B-roll clips and 1 carousel a day. Roles include planning the day and locking scripts, finding viral trends, editing A- and B-roll, picking topics and editing each long-form channel, writing X copy, prepping a newsletter and a weekday news roundup, handling home busywork, Gmail and Calendar, tracking a Coast FIRE plan, and a bot that designs and fixes the other bots.
