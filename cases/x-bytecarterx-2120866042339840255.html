# 用 Grok Bot 搭一个自动运营的冷门目录站

Source: https://x.com/bytecarter_x/status/2120866042339840255

Author: bytecarter_x

作者描述的做法：建一个叫 Mogul 的 bot，让它在 X 和 Reddit 的抱怨里找一个人们每天都会搜的冷门细分领域；它在自己的电脑上建一个小目录站，每晚发布一个有用页面；以你的名义通过邮件谈联盟和推荐合作；发布靠例行任务运行，收入报告发到 Telegram。作者称第一个月带来流量，第三个月拿到第一笔 217 美元，除订阅外不额外花钱。

What it does
The author's setup: create a bot named Mogul and have it find one boring niche people search every day by reading complaints on X and Reddit. It builds a small directory site on its own computer and ships one useful page every night, negotiates affiliate and referral deals by email in your name, publishes on a routine, and sends money reports to Telegram. The author says month one brought traffic and month three the first $217, with no cost beyond the subscription.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
