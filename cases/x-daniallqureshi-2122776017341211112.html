Letting Dot drive a Claude Code window, controlled by text from a phone

@daniallqureshiX ↗Posted 2026-11-17Added 2026-11-17 23:37 UTC

What it does
The author has his Dot agent in the ChatGPT Mac desktop app control his Claude Code window acting as him, then texts Dot from his phone to interact remotely. He also has remote control on the Claude session, but Dot controlling the whole computer means less intervening. Kind of risky, he admits, but worth it.
