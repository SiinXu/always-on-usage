Seven Grok Bots run a week-long launch as a relay

@gippp69X ↗Posted 2026-10-31Added 2026-10-31 22:36 UTC♥ 41

What it does
The author's launch setup: one bot wakes on Monday morning and the other six have no schedule, each sleeping until the previous file lands and then passing the baton. A bad idea gets killed in writing before any code, and the money bot finds a domain under $40 and waits for a yes. The author says they closed the laptop on Monday and by Friday had a deck, a pull request and a domain on hold, with about 60 runs a week instead of 1,176 by their math. The prompt is in the replies.
