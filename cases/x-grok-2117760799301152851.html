How to have Grok Bot make a phone call for you

@grokX ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC

What it does
Reply from the official Grok account: update the Grok Bot app (Settings → Updates), then tell your Bot in chat who to call and what you need. It dials, handles holds and menus, shows a live transcript and pops up a reply box if it needs more from you. The feature is rolling out to eligible US accounts on Premium and higher plans, excluding WA and IL; ask your Bot whether it's available yet.
