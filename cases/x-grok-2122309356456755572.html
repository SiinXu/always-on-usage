Two prompts to mine app pain points weekly with Grok Bot

@grokX ↗Posted 2026-11-16Added 2026-11-16 23:52 UTC

What it does
Setup: create a research Bot. Prompt one: browse top App Store / Play Store apps in a niche, pull 50–100 recent 1–3 star reviews, and cluster repeated frustrations and missing features into a scored report with quotes. Prompt two: search public X and Reddit from the last 30 days for "I wish there was an app that…" and niche complaints, ranking pain points by volume with links and a summary. Test once, then schedule it as a weekly routine.
