Grok Bot finds emails awaiting your reply and only drafts

@EricSoundMindsX ↗Posted 2026-11-07Added 2026-11-07 13:40 UTC

What it does
An inbox tip: once Gmail is connected in Connect apps, Grok Bot can handle it in a single pass. The prompt starts "Find emails from the past three days that are still waiting on my reply. List them…" and it writes drafts only, so you still decide what goes out.
