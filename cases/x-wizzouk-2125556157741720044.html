# 给 Dot 一个邮箱，像同事一样派活

Source: https://x.com/wizzouk/status/2125556157741720044

Author: wizzouk

What it does
The author suggests giving Dot its own email address. Their chief-of-staff bot, Dr Eggbot, hands out jobs by email, collects replies and logs them in an external task tracker. Dot doesn't need to be inside Grok Bot; it needs a clear inbox and a clear job.
