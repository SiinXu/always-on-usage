Don't build a bot team on day one: one topic, one bot

@AndresRuggiero3X ↗Posted 2026-10-20Added 2026-10-20 22:41 UTC

What it does
The author calls it the most common Grok Bot overcomplication: separate bills, subscriptions, budget and reminder bots for home finances. One money bot can find subscriptions, flag unused ones, remind you before bills are due and send a weekly summary. It keeps all the context, uses fewer tokens and has one set of instructions. Split only when the job is genuinely different.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
