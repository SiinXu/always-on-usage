# 用 iPhone 经营小店：截图 Line 聊天，让 Grok 建预约

Source: https://x.com/TheKnudsen/status/2121937792695468311

Author: TheKnudsen

作者几乎已能只用 iPhone 经营生意：Tailscale 连到预约服务器，日历显示在店里的 iPad 上；客户在 Line 上预约，他截图聊天，由 Grok 创建预约。最后缺的一步是给员工发短信，他希望支持 iOS 上的 iMessage。

What it does
The author can almost run the business from his iPhone: Tailscale reaches the booking server and the calendar shows on the shop iPad. Customers book on Line, so he screenshots the chat and Grok makes the booking. The missing step is texting staff; he asks for iMessage on iOS.
