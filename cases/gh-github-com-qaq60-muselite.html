Steps / points from the source
  1. Runner: macos-latest (Xcode 26.6, iOS SDK 26.5), xcodebuild archive with CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO
  2. Output: MuseLite-unsigned.ipa artifact + SHA-256
  3. Download MuseLite-unsigned.ipa from the Actions artifact (or a Release).
  4. Sign & install with Sideloadly, AltStore, Feather, ESign, or
  5. Trust the developer profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  6. Launch Muse and sign in with your Meta account.
  7. Full-screen WKWebView, no chrome, viewport locked to device width
  8. applicationNameForUserAgent mimics a real Safari tab (avoids "unsupported browser" gates)
