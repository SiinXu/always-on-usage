esp32-dot: a desk touchscreen companion for your Dot

rsmsGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-08Added 2026-10-15 13:40 UTC★ 10

What it does
A small physical interface for ChatGPT Dot that shows messages, asks you to choose between options and sends voice messages; native Rust firmware, with a computer-side bridge handling the queue, local speech recognition and the MCP connection. The ESP32 runs no model.
