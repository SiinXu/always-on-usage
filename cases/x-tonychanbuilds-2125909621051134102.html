# 帖子爆火当天，用 Grok Bot 排出该先回复谁

Source: https://x.com/TonyChanBuilds/status/2125909621051134102

Author: TonyChanBuilds

作者的帖子被 Elon Musk 转发，获得 38.6 万次浏览、7.2 万次认证用户曝光。面对数百条回复，他让 Grok Bot 告诉他先回复谁，再对每个人提一个真实的问题或想法；他的 bots 还帮他在同一天上线了一份 newsletter。他说那天只发了 5 条帖子，每条都超过 1000 次浏览。

What it does
After Elon Musk reposted the author, the post reached 386K views and 72K verified impressions. Facing hundreds of replies, the author had Grok Bot say who to reply to first, then asked each person a real question or thought; the author's bots also helped launch a newsletter the same day. The author says each of the 5 posts that day passed 1,000 views.
