What it does
MIT demo for an AI system design tutorial: a mobile-first Next.js custom-cake shop that Grok Bot built from GitHub issues while being driven from a phone, deployed to Vercel, with orders carried in HMAC-signed links instead of a database, Accept and Decline emails to the bakery, and a stubbed PayPal invoice step.

Steps / points from the source
  1. Production deploys automatically from main to https://nextjs-cake-shop-grok-bot.vercel.app.
  2. Preview deployments are created automatically for every pull request; Vercel posts the preview link on the PR.
  3. The build needs no secrets. At runtime, order requests need ORDER_SECRET (set on Vercel for Production and Preview). Gmail sending is optional; without GMAIL
  4. The cart is saved in the browser (localStorage).

  6. The order lives in signed links: the order is compact JSON (deflated, base64url) in ?d=, and ?sig= is an HMAC-SHA256 with ORDER_SECRET over the purpos
  7. nodemailer sends two emails through Gmail: to BAKERY_EMAIL with signed Accept / Decline links, and to the customer with a link to their order page. Without
  8. Accept (/order//accept) shows the order and one button, "Confirm this order". Confirming emails the customer that the order is confirmed, with a link to the

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
