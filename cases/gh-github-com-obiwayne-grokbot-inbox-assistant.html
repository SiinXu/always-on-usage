grokbot-inbox-assistant: email summaries and reply drafts, approved in Telegram

ObiwayneGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-22Added 2026-10-22 12:38 UTC

What it does
An open-source template: each new email gets a summary, a next action and a reply draft that you approve in Telegram. Built on n8n, AgentMail and the Grok API, with a Grok Bot pack.

Steps / points from the source
  1. Classifies it: customer enquiry, lead, invoice, newsletter, urgent or other.
  2. Summarises it in up to three sentences and suggests one next action.
  3. Drafts a reply using only your business profile: prices, hours, tone, rules. Missing facts become [[ASK: …]] placeholders, never guesses. No drafts for newsl……
  4. Saves the draft in AgentMail as a reply in the same thread.
  5. Messages you on Telegram with the summary and the draft, plus ✅ Send reply and 🗑 Discard buttons. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
  6. n8n: free self-hosted for your own business use (Sustainable Use License), or n8n Cloud.
  7. AgentMail: free for 3 inboxes and 100 emails a day; $20 a month adds your own domain.
