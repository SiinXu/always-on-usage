Muse price-checked a grocery basket across three quick-commerce apps and ordered

@CaffinatedAKX ↗Posted 2026-09-27Added 2026-10-15 03:19 UTC♥ 1

What it does
Muse built a cart on Zepto and compared the same basket on Swiggy Instamart and BigBasket. ₹10 short of free delivery, it followed the author's no-delivery-fee rule and added a staple ranked from 24 past orders. Zepto won at ₹239 with 6-minute delivery. The author signed up via VPN in India and paid with Amazon Pay.
