trinity-cli: one CLI over three always-on agents

akari-soraGitHub ↗Posted 2026-11-08Added 2026-11-08 13:39 UTC

What it does
An unofficial open-source CLI over three always-on agents (ChatGPT Your dot, Grok Bot, Meta Muse), with a migrate command to move a Grok Bot to Muse or Your dot; it wraps chatgpt-dot-cli, grok-bot-cli (gbot) and muse-web-cli and is not affiliated with OpenAI, xAI, Cursor or Meta.
