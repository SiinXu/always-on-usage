Grok Bot searched 2 years of iMessages and found $425.60 owed

@ScottyBeamIOX ↗Posted 2026-11-11Added 2026-11-11 23:43 UTC♥ 37

What it does
After connecting Messages, the author had Grok Bot search two years of iMessages for money people owed him. It listed six debts totalling $425.60, such as $212 for a Paris Airbnb split and $89 for a concert ticket, each with the exact message and date. He then had it draft a friendly reminder for each person and send only the ones he approved. The full prompt is in the post.
