Mr. Shorts, a free Grok Bot template for editing short videos

@smsonxX ↗Posted 2026-10-31Added 2026-10-31 22:36 UTC♥ 6

What it does
The author released the bot they use for every Short as a free template on the Grok Bot Marketplace. It asks a few setup questions to match your brand, cuts dead air and tightens pacing, captions in your style, puts names and logos on screen as you say them, builds the thumbnail from a real frame, and writes titles, captions and hashtags for YouTube, TikTok, Reels and X. You record, it edits, you approve; nothing goes live without your OK.
