What it does
The author's checklist: is the routine active; its schedule and the time zone it actually runs in; whether the trigger is too narrow (or the channel too noisy); and hit Test and read the result. You can also ask Grok Bot to check a named routine against these 4 points and only report which one failed, without fixing anything.
