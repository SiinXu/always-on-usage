What it does
The author reviews three promises from a week ago: Nov 9, "dramatic improvement by tomorrow", yet a Super user reports 45% used in 6 hours on one mid-sized project; Nov 10, Grok 4.8 soft rollout to Bot users that week, still not officially released by Nov 16; Nov 14, efficiency improvements, which shipped: about 7% more usage, up to about 15% on routine runs. The original post drew 10,287 replies, with top replies complaining about reduced usage.
