User comparison: Grok Bot runs more and more tasks, rated above Muse and Claude Duet

@chris_richardeX ↗Posted 2026-11-15Added 2026-11-15 23:46 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author says he uses Grok Bot to manage more and more tasks, including managing his Opus builds as a design director, rating it 10/10 and well ahead of Muse. He also has Claude Duet but says it needs more features and can't read files on his own computer when he's away. For scheduling and calendars he built his own agent with an AI persona that can reach out and accept calendar requests, running on its own version of Slack.
