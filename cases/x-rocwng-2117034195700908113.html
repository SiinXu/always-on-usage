What it does
The post retells Lauren (@poteto)'s advice: use dr eggbot to design bots; keep a few good bots rather than many; save what you teach as skills, optionally in a private GitHub repo; add connectors and the vault, and let a bot open its own inbox to sign up for its own accounts; onboard it like an intern and ask what it can automate; dump ideas by voice and ask it to restate them. Extras: scheduled routines at most hourly, filter event triggers, have bots write scripts to save tokens, and share bots in Slack on team plans.

Risks and limits
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
