What it does
The author notes how Grok Bot routines work: tell the Bot in chat what to do and when, for example "Every weekday at 9:00 AM, summarize new support tickets in this chat", and it appears under Details > Routines. A new routine doesn't run when you create it; it waits for its next scheduled time.
