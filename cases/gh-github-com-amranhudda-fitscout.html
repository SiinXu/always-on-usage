Steps / points from the source
  1. Photos: 50 photos produced 150 garments. My face was matched on-device in 48 of the 50, including 27 group shots.
  2. Tuning agent: test-ran 6 searches and rewrote 5 overly specific ones into the words sellers actually use in titles, in 21 seconds for $0.16.
  3. First scan: pulled 131 Grailed listings and judged 130 in 103 seconds. It flagged 22 as worth showing and sent the best 6 as notifications.
  4. Size check: XXL and M versions of a sweater were marked as the wrong size for "L/XL".
  5. Course correction: the top pick was a $43 black Polo quarter-zip at 9.5/10, which is basically one I already own. I said I want new things inspired by my sty……
  6. After the correction: the next scan judged 199 listings in 156 seconds. The top picks were a vintage cream Aran wool cardigan ($64), a gray chunky cable-knit……
  7. Cost: the whole first night, including building and testing, used $3.12 of OpenAI. A hard daily cap ($2 by default, $5 here) stops the AI calls if it's reached.…
  8. Face matching stays on the phone. The cloud model only sees a copy of the photo with a green box around me, so it never has to recognize anyone. My reference……
