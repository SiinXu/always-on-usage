What it does
Pick a city and niche, find buyers asking for help and qualified providers in Facebook Groups, Marketplace and Instagram, clean and rank the data, match both sides and take a success fee. Example: night nurses in Montreal under $30/hour. Costs: free Muse tier, the $100/month plan, about $15 for a Bland AI phone plan. Limit: Muse won't find the best source on its own; you must tell it where to search and what to prioritize.
