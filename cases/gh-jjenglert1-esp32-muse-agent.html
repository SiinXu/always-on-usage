Steps / points from the source
  1. Watch the demo on X
  2. 🗣️ **A voice agent in your pocket.** Hold the button, talk, let go. Your Muse agent answers.
  3. 🔊 **It talks back.** Muse gadgets normally show replies as text only. This guide adds a real voice.
  4. 😊 **An animated face** on a bright AMOLED touchscreen.
  5. 🔋 **Battery powered** (optional), so it works anywhere on Wi-Fi.
  6. 🧠 **Your own Muse agent**, the same one in the Muse phone app, with your memory and tools.
  7. A **USB-C cable that carries data** (not a charge-only cable)
  8. A **Mac or Linux computer**
