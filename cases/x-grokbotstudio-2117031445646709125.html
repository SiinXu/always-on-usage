4 principles for building an AI staff team with Grok Bot (Japanese summary)

@grokbot_studioX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 23:42 UTC

What it does
The author condenses methods shared by overseas Grok Bot users into 4 principles: decide roles before headcount and split bots only for work with different goals or owners; turn taught work into skills, saving them once the steps are stable after a trial; grant only the permissions needed, deciding up front which connectors to use and which actions need approval; and keep routines narrow in interval and scope, starting with wide intervals and limiting who gets notified.
