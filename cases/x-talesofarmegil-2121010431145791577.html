Giving Grok Bots the goal, not just the task

@talesofarmegilX ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 23:47 UTC

What it does
The author usually gives his bots tasks. One evening he opened a separate group chat with just his personal advisor bot and a startup-builder bot on trial, limited to one topic, an online tutor idea, and told them his long-term goal: living off the family's own ideas and products, with something online that needs very little of his time. Now every task has a direction, and a suggestion that eats more of his evenings is off course.
