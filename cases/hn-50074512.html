What it does
The author wrote nine original characters for Meta Muse, each with SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, NAMES.md and artwork, open-sourced on GitHub. Her finding: Muse struggled to adopt a given persona or even follow basic preferences like writing style, while Claude follows soul.md files well in her experience. She is unsure why; results may vary.
