Grok Bot Mac “unavailable” after password reset (expired session)

RongleCat/awesome-grok-bot (GitHub) ↗

Title and summary are in the original language, not translated.

What it does
Staff (deanrie): After a password reset, Grok Bot on Mac can keep an expired session and show “isn’t available on this account yet” even though SuperGrok/Cursor access and your cloud computer/Bots are fine — Log out from the bottom of that screen, fully Quit (Cmd+Q), reopen and Sign in with the new password; Check Access→download is a misleading expired-session UI, not a missing entitlement.
