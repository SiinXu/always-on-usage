# 让 agent 用独立邮箱替你跟车商砍价

Source: https://x.com/alpha404ai/status/2125551881355251802

Author: alpha404ai

帖子介绍 CarEdge 的做法：买家选车后，agent 开独立邮箱和电话号码联系多家车商，只要书面落地价，用每份报价压下一家；据称已部署 1 万多次，平均省 1000 多美元和约 5 小时，有位买家省了 4000 美元。作者认为给 Claude、Grok Bot、Gemini 等任一 agent 独立收件箱和清晰简报也能照做，并附简报模板。

What it does
The post describes CarEdge's approach: after the buyer picks a car, an agent gets its own email and phone number, contacts dealers, insists on a written out-the-door price and uses each quote against the next. It reports 10,000+ deployments, over $1,000 and about 5 hours saved on average, and one buyer $4,000 up. The author says any agent (Claude, Grok Bot, Gemini and others) with a separate inbox and a clear brief can do the same, and shares a brief.
