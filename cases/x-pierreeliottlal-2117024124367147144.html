What it does
GojiberryAI released a Sales OS for Grok Bot with 13 agent roles (Head of Sales, Signal Hunter, ICP Analyst, Account Researcher, Lead Enricher, Intent Scorer, LinkedIn Copywriter, Outreach Operator, Reply Agent, Follow-up Agent, Meeting Qualifier, Pipeline Analyst, Sales Manager), each connected to the GojiberryAI MCP. They hand work along a chain: spot someone who engaged with a competitor, check fit, research, enrich contact data, write the message, reach out, handle the reply and move them toward a demo. The install link is shared to people who comment "BOT".

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
