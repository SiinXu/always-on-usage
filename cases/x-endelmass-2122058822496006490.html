A weekly 'screen time report' for 7 Grok Bot agents: inbox, support, trends, threads, leads, bugs

@endelmassX ↗Posted 2026-11-15Added 2026-11-15 23:46 UTC♥ 10

What it does
The author's own iPhone report showed 3h 41m a day, mostly scrolling. He opened the same kind of report for his 7 Grok Bot agents: Sorter sorts his inbox and flags only what needs him; Support Desk answers tickets from his docs and holds refunds for his ok; Scout watches trends and drafts post angles; Threadwriter turns them into thread drafts for review; Booker follows up leads and books calls only after he accepts; Bug Hunter opens fixes as PRs but never merges; Verifier tests every fix before a merge. The last card shows his own time, including every approval, since nothing is sent without his ok.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
