What it does
The author says he created 6 Grok Bots that close 37 of his 50 daily tasks before he opens his laptop. By his numbers they cost $0.41 a day, versus $1,640 a month for an assistant doing the same work. The structure follows one rule: inbox → chief bot → 5 workers → reports → back to inbox.
