# 一条提示词，让 Grok Bot 找出刚融资且在招工程师的初创公司

Source: https://x.com/paarangatrai/status/2125550046985023643

Author: paarangatrai

作者用一条提示词让 Grok Bot 找出近 60 天融资（从 600 万美元种子前轮到 4500 万美元 A 轮）且在招工程师的 5 家初创公司，并拉出创始人的 LinkedIn、X、GitHub、验证过的工作邮箱和过往经历。整次运行花费 7 美分。

What it does
With one prompt, Grok Bot found 5 startups that raised in the last 60 days (from a $6M pre-seed to a $45M Series A) and are hiring engineers, then pulled each founder's LinkedIn, X, GitHub, a verified work email and prior work. The whole run cost 7 cents.
