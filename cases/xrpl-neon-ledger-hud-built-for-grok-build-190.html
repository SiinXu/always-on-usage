Steps / points from the source
  1. Subscribes to public XRPL WebSockets (ledger + transactions)
  2. Breaks down each validated close by transaction type
  3. WebGL energy field + additive particles on close
  4. Holo archive of the highest 100 closes (localStorage)
  5. Click a ranked ledger for the projected dossier
  6. Filters, pause, families view, bloom, keyboard shortcuts
