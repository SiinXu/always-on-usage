Use +aliases on a Grok Bot inbox to wake different routines

@its_LtDanX ↗Posted 2026-11-07Added 2026-11-07 13:40 UTC♥ 1

What it does
Same Grok Bot inbox, different tags, and each tag can wake its own routine: name+invoices@ wakes the one that files bills, name+leads@ wakes the one that drafts the follow-up. Mail to a tag nobody listens to wakes nothing.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
