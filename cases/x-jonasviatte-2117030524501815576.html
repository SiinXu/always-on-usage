In the author's words

The author's post, quoted verbatim and left untranslated.

Prompt
Set up a daily routine that protects my Grok Bot usage limit.

Each run:
1. Check my current usage percentage, the reset date, and how many days are left until the reset.
2. Work out my pace (usage so far divided by days elapsed in this period) and project my usage at the reset date.
3. If the projection stays under 100%, do nothing and stay completely silent. No message, no question.
4. If the projection goes over 100%:
   - Send me one short message with my current %, the days left, and my projected % at reset.
   - List my bots' regular tasks and routines, ranked by how much usage each one takes.
   - Suggest which non-urgent tasks to throttle, make less frequent, or defer until after the reset so I land under 100%.
   - Ask me which tasks to keep as priorities and which to pause. Only pause or change something after I say yes, and resume paused tasks once the usage resets.

Goal: my usage never hits 100% before the reset, and I only hear from you when the numbers say I need to.
