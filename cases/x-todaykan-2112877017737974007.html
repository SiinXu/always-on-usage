What it does
The author says the easiest beginner mistake is building 20 bots at once. In week one they pinned only two things: an afternoon daily-post routine and an evening review routine, to prove the loop of the bot waking itself, writing files and them only reading results before trying multi-bot work. They point to a four-step roadmap post as a table of contents.
