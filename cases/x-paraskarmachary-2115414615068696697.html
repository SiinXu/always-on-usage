Five tips for researchers setting up an always-on agent

@paraskarmacharyX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-29 12:46 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author says they finished April travel reimbursements six months late and ChatGPT cleared the pile in one sitting. Their five tips: sort tasks into Do (easy to undo, like filing receipts), Digest (weekly, new papers), Ping (urgent and rare, like a retraction of a cited paper) and Ask (anything sent in your name); every extra alert makes the urgent one easier to miss; a weekly scheduled search is enough for literature; what it can see matters more than how smart it is (theirs reads personal Gmail, not the institutional inbox); and start with a job where mistakes are easy to undo. They note Dots isn't on ChatGPT Plus and Claude Duet is a US waitlist.
