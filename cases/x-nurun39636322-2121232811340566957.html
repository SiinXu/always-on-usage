A DIY home battery that Grok Bot runs like a Powerwall

@nurun39636322X ↗Posted 2026-11-13Added 2026-11-13 13:39 UTC♥ 1

What it does
No Tesla Powerwall is sold where the author lives, so he built his own: a 12 kW Deye hybrid inverter, a ~30 kWh LFP battery and 7.5 kW of solar panels for now. He designed it with Grok's help; a structural engineer checked the structure and licensed electricians checked the wiring. Every evening Grok Bot checks tomorrow's forecast and sets the battery floor: 50% if sunny, 65% if cloudy, and it charges ahead of an outage. Monitoring and automation run over Starlink Wi-Fi. Projected: up to ~15% less grid power on sunny days.
