Five tips for researchers setting up an always-on agent

@paraskarmacharyX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 22:52 UTC

What it does
A researcher's advice: sort jobs into Do (easy to undo, like filing receipts), weekly Digest, rare urgent Ping, and Ask for anything sent in your name; keep the Ping lane tiny since extra alerts hide urgent ones; a scheduled weekly search is enough for literature updates; what it can see matters more than how smart it is, so his reads personal Gmail, not his institutional inbox; and start with jobs that are easy to undo.
