What it does
The author set up one account with a team of Grok Bot agents, one job each, and reads a morning digest: 52 inbound leads scored and routed, 36 outbound sequences queued with 0 sent, inbox at zero with 5 replies parked, revenue +4.2% versus the 7-day, 9 receipts filed and 1 flagged, 11 tickets closed and 2 escalated.
