Grok Bot running a company's night shift

What it does
The author set up one account with a team of Grok Bot agents, one job each, and reads a morning digest: 52 inbound leads scored and routed, 36 outbound sequences queued with 0 sent, inbox at zero with 5 replies parked, revenue +4.2% versus the 7-day, 9 receipts filed and 1 flagged, 11 tickets closed and 2 escalated. Nothing is sent, spent or posted until they approve at 07:00. They say it runs on Opus 5.5 inside Grok Bot, with prompts and config in the article.

Risks and limits
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
