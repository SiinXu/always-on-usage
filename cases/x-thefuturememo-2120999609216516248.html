What it does
The post explains that the bot opens Gmail, Slack, Notion, Calendar, GitHub and a CRM in turn, does the job and moves on. Each bot gets its own cloud computer with a browser, a terminal and files, and logs into apps the way a person would, so work keeps going after you close the tab.
