# Grok Bot 不是加了插件的聊天，而是有自己的电脑

Source: https://x.com/TheFutureMemo/status/2120999609216516248

Author: TheFutureMemo

帖子解释：bot 会依次打开 Gmail、Slack、Notion、日历、GitHub、CRM，做完一件就换下一件。每个 bot 有自己的云端电脑，带浏览器、终端和文件，像人一样登录应用，所以关掉标签页后工作仍在继续。帖子称截至 11 月 6 日，xAI 美国定价页把它列在每月 30 美元的 Premium 方案里。

What it does
The post explains that the bot opens Gmail, Slack, Notion, Calendar, GitHub and a CRM in turn, does the job and moves on. Each bot gets its own cloud computer with a browser, a terminal and files, and logs into apps the way a person would, so work keeps going after you close the tab. As of Nov 6, it says, xAI's US pricing page lists it inside the $30/month Premium plan.
