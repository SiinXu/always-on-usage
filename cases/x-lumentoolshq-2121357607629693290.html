Dots Custom rules: start on "ask first"

@LumenToolsHQX ↗Posted 2026-11-13Added 2026-11-13 23:40 UTC

What it does
The author says the key Dots setting is Custom rules: for each kind of action you choose take action without asking, take action if pre-approved, ask before taking action, or hand off to you; steps like changing a password always stay with you. He suggests starting on "ask first" with one low-stakes job, watching it for a week, then loosening.
