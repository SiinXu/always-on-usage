# 航班取消后用 6 个 Grok Bot 拿回现金退款

Source: https://x.com/misat0x/status/2125575575234281489

Author: misat0x

作者的姐姐在 Delta 系统故障中航班被取消，拒绝了柜台给的 150 美元代金券，让 6 个 Grok Bot 依据 Delta 自己的规则（及美国 14 CFR Part 260 退款规定）处理。到周三早上，836.40 美元退回卡里，含 612.40 美元机票、酒店、Uber 和晚餐。

What it does
During a Delta system outage the author's sister had her flight cancelled, refused a $150 voucher at the desk and set 6 Grok Bots on Delta's own rules (and the US 14 CFR Part 260 refund rule). By Wednesday morning $836.40 was back on her card: the $612.40 ticket plus hotel, Ubers and dinner.

  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
