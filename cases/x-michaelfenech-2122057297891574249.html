# 给 Grok Bot 团队第一天就该下的 4 条常驻指令

Source: https://x.com/Michael_Fenech_/status/2122057297891574249

Author: Michael_Fenech_

作者给出一段可直接发给 Chief of Staff 的提示，让它把 4 条常驻指令推到所有 bot：1）会变的日期、价格、数字、状态先查负责它的已连接应用，查不到就直说；2）完成后给出文件、记录或确认的链接，没做完的部分说清楚；3）说明检查是否真的成功，比如查了哪个收件箱、往前查多久，收件箱没加载就说没查成；4）可逆的工作直接做，发送、花钱、删除或承诺前确认已获授权。最后让它汇报哪些 bot 已更新、哪些还需处理。

What it does
The author shares a prompt to paste into your Chief of Staff that rolls out 4 standing instructions across the bot team: 1) check the connected app that owns any changeable date, price, number or status, and say so if it can't be verified; 2) show the result with a link to the file, record or confirmation, and name any part still open; 3) say whether the check worked, such as which inbox was searched and how far back, or that the inbox didn't load; 4) do reversible work inside the brief, but confirm authorisation before sending, spending, deleting or committing. It ends by asking which bots were updated and which still need attention.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
