What it does
The post collects self-reported Muse uses from X users: calling to cut phone and internet bills (AT&T from $80 to $40; Peter Yang reported saving over $800 a year), re-shopping car insurance, getting a $250 Delta credit in about 5 minutes after a 7-hour delay, watching booked fares for drops, finding over $1,500 in state unclaimed property in about 5 minutes, and auditing unused subscriptions. Others include booking restaurants for a Japan trip and negotiating in Japanese with a Hakone hotel, Facebook Marketplace selling, Instacart orders and bulk job applications. The author notes most are self-reports and some sites such as Amazon block it.
