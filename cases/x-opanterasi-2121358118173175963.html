Counting how often you chase your assistant: 29/141 down to 12/145

@opanterasiX ↗Posted 2026-11-13Added 2026-11-13 23:40 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author counted messages chasing her AI assistant: 29 of 141 from 10/30 to 11/05, and 12 of 145 this week. Of 18 chases right after a delivery, 15 were the same issue: the page was created but never reached her. The fix was a check that the page actually appears in the conversation, re-showing it before any message goes out, with the count rerun weekly.
