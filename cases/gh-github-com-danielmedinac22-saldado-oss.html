saldado-oss: connect your Grok Bot to WhatsApp

danielmedinac22GitHub ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC

What it does
The open-source project saldado-oss connects your Grok Bot to WhatsApp. You can self-host it with your own Kapso or use the hosted version at saldadoapp.com; it is built with Next.js.

Steps / points from the source
  1. 🧩 Bursts become one message. Three texts in a row reach your agent as one.
  2. 🔁 Nothing gets lost. If your agent is slow or down, Saldado retries.
  3. 🚨 You hear about failures. If Meta rejects a message, the owner gets told why, in plain words.
  4. 📲 Signup over WhatsApp. Bring your own number, or get a new one.
  5. 🔐 Secrets encrypted at rest. Kapso keys, webhook secrets and agent tokens are all encrypted in the database.
  6. Node.js 20+ and pnpm.
  7. A Postgres database. The app uses @neondatabase/serverless, so Neon is the easiest option (the free tier is fine).
  8. A Kapso account and project, with its API key.
