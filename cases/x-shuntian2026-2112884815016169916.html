Grok Bot lesson 1: what to do right after installing

@shuntian2026X ↗Posted 2026-10-21Added 2026-10-21 12:42 UTC

What it does
The author wrote a first beginner lesson on what to do after installing Grok Bot. First, turn off computer permissions you don't need: the desktop app has two switches, run on this computer and route traffic through this computer, so keep them off until needed. Second, give the bot a job description: what it does, what it delivers, where it delivers it, and when it must stop and ask you. The third step is not in the excerpt.
