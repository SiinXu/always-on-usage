What it does
When unhappy, the author reacts 👎 and explains what they expected; the Muse (HUO) logs the feedback in a living Google Sheet, turns corrections into instructions and reflects overnight to adjust. Setting it up took just asking plus some tweaks; failure logs and the daily reflection file are shown.
