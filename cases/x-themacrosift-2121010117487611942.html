What it does
Your dot now follows custom instructions: tell it how to behave or which actions need your OK first, such as "show me a draft before sending any emails." Set it in the thread, in Customize > Behavior on mobile, or from the dot's menu on web and desktop. OpenAI also says replies are faster, with less jargon and better follow-through.
