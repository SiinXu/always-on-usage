Tailscale official: put Muse on your tailnet and fence it with access controls

Andrew CunninghamHacker News · Tailscale ↗Posted 2026-09-30Added 2026-10-15 03:19 UTC▲ 1

What it does
Via its connector, Muse joins your tailnet as its own node: it can check node status, use self-hosted services and run commands over Tailscale SSH (the author checked podman containers and updated a Raspberry Pi). It only makes outbound connections and asks for confirmation the first time it reaches a device; grants and tags let you wall it off to what you explicitly allow.
