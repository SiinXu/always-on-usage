What it does
Muse found the author's renters policy had been canceled and was overpriced, logged into GEICO to review it, submitted 6 quote comparisons and built a coverage-and-cost table with a recommendation. After he confirmed payment via his Link account, he had a new policy $60/year cheaper — all while shopping at Costco.
