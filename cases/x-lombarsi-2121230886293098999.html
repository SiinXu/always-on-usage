Grok Bot gathers defamation evidence into a legal file

@LombarsiX ↗Posted 2026-11-13Added 2026-11-13 13:39 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author asked Grok Bot to find every tweet where a person made clear he was ignoring the defamation claim or doubling down. Grok Bot found 14 tweets, saved them, added them to his 14-page legal PDF for a lawyer, and built a Google Drive with the screenshots. It is now scanning the person's 1,300+ YouTube videos for more, which the author says is taking a full week.
