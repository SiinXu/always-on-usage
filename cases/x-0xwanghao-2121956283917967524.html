What it does
The author keeps a note, on-hold.md, with who to call, why, the account number and the desired outcome. Grok Bot dials, works the phone menu and waits; when a person picks up it rings his phone, and if he doesn't answer in 30 seconds it asks for a callback time and adds it to Calendar. Afterwards it sends a 3-line recap in Messages and puts the promise in Reminders. Rules: never confirm payments, cancellations or fees, never read out card or ID numbers, say it's an assistant first, one open call per company.
