What it does
Staff: Grok Bot’s cloud computer cannot join a corporate VPN today (don’t install a VPN client on it — that can knock the box offline); Atlassian Cloud / public SSO works via browser takeover (Okta etc.), YubiKey works on macOS/Windows under Settings → Security Key, and internal VPN-only sites need Execution on Local Computer on your VPN’d machine.
