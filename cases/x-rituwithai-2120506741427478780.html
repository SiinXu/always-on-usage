Demo then skill: Grok Bot used 40.6% fewer tokens on the same task

@RituWithAIX ↗Posted 2026-11-11Added 2026-11-11 23:43 UTC♥ 5

What it does
The author ran one task twice: find a specific phone colour and storage online, check delivery to Sydney postcode 2000, and report the date or out-of-stock status. With instructions only, Grok Bot worked out the steps itself and used 1,316,200 tokens. He then recorded himself doing the flow on the bot's computer and had it turn the recording into a reusable skill; the same task then used 782,200 tokens, 40.6% less. He notes it was one experiment and building the skill also cost usage.
