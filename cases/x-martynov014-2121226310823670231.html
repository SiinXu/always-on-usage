Giving Grok Bot a body: "Elon Man" turns its head when you walk in

@martynov014X ↗Posted 2026-11-13Added 2026-11-13 13:39 UTC♥ 18

What it does
The author built "Elon Man" for Grok Bot: a camera head, a face that sleeps and talks, and a neck that turns when someone walks in. Bank access is read-only and it asks before it sends, pays, posts or trades. One morning in the video: a failed print paused overnight, a new subscription flagged but not cancelled, 2 jobs waiting for approval, and $0 moved without him. His rule: every morning at 8, report what was finished and what needs him, and never send, pay, post or trade without his OK.
