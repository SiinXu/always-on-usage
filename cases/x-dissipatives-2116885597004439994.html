Grok Bot's Teach a task: record a workflow, get a skill draft

@DissipativeSX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 13:46 UTC

What it does
The author explains Teach a task: in the bot's computer view, click the button, state the goal, do the task once in its browser, and stop to get a skill draft. Recording is capped at 10 minutes with no mic, and the draft misses rules and failure handling. Routine pitfalls: new schedules don't run until the next slot (Test runs for real and uses quota), timezone follows bot settings, and limits are 300 runs/hour total, 200 per routine, with no auto-retry.

Risks and limits
  • Uses a browser or website login: the costliest kind of step, so prefer a connector where one exists
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
