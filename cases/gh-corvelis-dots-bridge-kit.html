Dots Bridge Kit: talk to your Dot through Even G2 glasses

CorvelisGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-10Added 2026-10-15 13:40 UTC★ 16

What it does
A local Mac bridge that links a Dot via a Chrome extension and offers text, notifications, speech recognition and calls over WebSocket; it ships an Even G2 glasses app to hold-to-talk or call the Dot and read replies on the lens.
