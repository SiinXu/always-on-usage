What it does
The author notes OpenAI Dots is still not properly available in the EU and suggests building your own always-on agent with the Claude or OpenAI API, a cron job and a Telegram bot: it checks your inbox, orders or ads each morning, drafts what needs doing and waits for your OK.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
