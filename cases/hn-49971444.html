Using Dots as a 7-agent swarm to improve a math bound (Lean-verified)

unexcitedneuronsHacker News · Substack ↗Posted 2026-10-05Added 2026-10-15 03:19 UTC▲ 3

What it does
The author split a Dot and 6 subagents into research, Lean formalization, computational search, adversarial review and coordination roles; in about 3 days they proved C(24,14,4) ≥ 20 (previously 19). The Lean proof passed the Palomar registry's checks but isn't peer-reviewed yet. Lessons: agents grind but don't know when to stop, verification is everything, and when the shared workspace was wiped they rebuilt it from messages.
