What it does
The author locked the woman from a reference image and generated four clips of about 10 seconds each with Meta Muse Video, then joined them. ffmpeg crossfades the seams, the whole piece is converted to 2.39:1 CinemaScope with a film-style grade, the free music is crossfaded too and loudness is normalized to -16 LUFS.
