What it does
A walkthrough of this week's new shopping feature: your bot makes a clickable Product Card, you open it, hover over a product photo and click Try On. The bot uses your selfie to make a 1024 × 1024 preview of you in the outfit, which you can edit, save or delete in the app; the selfie can be changed.
