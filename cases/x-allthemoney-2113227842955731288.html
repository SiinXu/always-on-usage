Quick Calendar Bot 3.0, a calendar bot for beginners

@allthemoneyX ↗Posted 2026-10-22Added 2026-10-22 22:41 UTC♥ 2

What it does
The author released Quick Calendar Bot 3.0 for people who have never used a Grok Bot agent. Say things like "dentist Tuesday at 2" or "remind me to pay rent on the 1st" and it adds them; you can move, cancel or ask "what’s on this week?". Setup is tap the link, add the bot, connect your calendar. The author says it is free.
