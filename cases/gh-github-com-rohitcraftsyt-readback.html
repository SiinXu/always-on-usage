What it does
The GitHub project readback catches readback errors between air traffic controllers and pilots using Meta's Muse models. It was built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026, tagged aviation safety and speech recognition.

Steps / points from the source
  1. Hearing the radio. Muse Voice Transcribe separates controller and pilot turns, and its keyword biasing gets the callsigns of aircraft that ADS-B says are act……
  2. Understanding clearances. Each turn becomes a structured clearance: callsign, instruction, altitude, heading, speed, frequency, runway, squawk.
  3. Two checks. The readback against the clearance, and the aircraft's ADS-B track against the clearance (for example, cleared to FL100 but still descending thro……
  4. On-premises mode. Air navigation providers can't send radio audio to a cloud service, so the parser also runs on Muse Glimmer (open weights).
  5. standard ICAO phraseology
  6. everyday variation: readbacks without keywords, stations, greetings, wind and taxi distractors, spelled ICAO codes
  7. real radio: clips cut off mid-word, hesitations, corrections, misheard words, airlines it has never seen
  8. stress: every kind of noise at once, more often
