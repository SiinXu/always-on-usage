One rule for Grok Bot teams: keep only bots that finish real work

@GetClareityX ↗Posted 2026-10-27Added 2026-10-27 22:37 UTC♥ 1

What it does
Some call Grok Bot teams a busy-work factory; the author says that's half right. Their rule: every bot must finish something they'd otherwise do themselves. Job search, deal alerts and fixing a TV setup pass; a bot that only produces more reports to read gets cut.
