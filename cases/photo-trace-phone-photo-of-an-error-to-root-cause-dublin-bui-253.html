What it does
Built with Grok Bot and Bronto at Grok Bot Dublin Builder Day on 4 Oct 2026: snap an error on your phone and the laptop shows the matching Bronto log search, one root cause and one fix command that only runs after a human approves.

Steps / points from the source
  1. reading the error and working out which service it came from,
  2. opening the log tool and writing the right query,
  3. scrolling through the matches to spot the pattern,
  4. remembering (or looking up) the command that fixes it.
  5. Join. Scan the QR code on the laptop with your phone. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi.
  6. Snap. Take a photo of the error on any screen.
  7. Read. analyse.py reads the text in the photo. It corrects the phone's rotation and retries at 90° and 270°.
  8. Match. The text is matched against errors/catalog.json, a catalogue of 13 common production failures. Each entry has signatures, a Bronto query, a cause, a
