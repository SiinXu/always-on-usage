Weeks with Grok Bot: memory problems undo finished work

@houseperuX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 23:41 UTC♥ 1

What it does
After several weeks, the author finds Grok Bot handles complex operations but stumbles on simple instructions. Even with a dedicated memory folder per bot, results stay mediocre; on a fairly simple app it repeats mistakes, and detail fixes on working code destroy earlier work. He argues the model puts its internal assumptions above explicit instructions.
