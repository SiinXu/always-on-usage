Two ways to book a meeting: Gemini in Google Chat vs Meta Muse

@MrAsifShahX ↗Posted 2026-11-11Added 2026-11-11 23:43 UTC

What it does
The author compares two ways an AI books meetings. Gemini in Google Chat: when someone types "free tomorrow afternoon?", it underlines it, suggests 3 times from your Calendar and flags conflicts; one click books it. Meta Muse now has its own email address (paid, US and Canada); CC it on a thread and it handles the replies and follow-ups.
