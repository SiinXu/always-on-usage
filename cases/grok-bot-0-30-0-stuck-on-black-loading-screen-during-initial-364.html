Grok Bot 0.30.0 Stuck on Black Loading Screen During Initial Setup on Mac

RongleCat/awesome-grok-bot (GitHub) ↗

Title and summary are in the original language, not translated.

What it does
Staff (Colin): Team admin role alone does not provision a Grok Bot computer — Grok Bot is included with a paid team seat (Standard or Premium), not the admin role. If the signed-in account has no seat, the app sits on the black loading screen with no computer to reset. Fix: assign yourself a seat in Cursor dashboard Members, fully quit and reopen Grok Bot; or sign in with a seated team account.
