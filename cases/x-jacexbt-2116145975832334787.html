A trading journal with Grok Bot and Notion

@j_acexbtX ↗Posted 2026-10-30Added 2026-10-30 12:46 UTC

What it does
The author connects Grok Bot to their trading account through an MT5 MCP server and to Notion, and has it log every position there with properties such as hit/miss, entry, take-profit and stop-loss. A separate trading-notes database holds topics they've learned; Grok Bot links the current market pattern to a related note or creates a new one. The author calls it a good workflow for learning to trade.
