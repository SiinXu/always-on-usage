# 把 Grok Bot 组成三人小队：发帖导师、执行官和学习教练

Source: https://x.com/harmoud_oussama/status/2125575547723915450

Author: harmoud_oussama

作者建了 3 个各司其职的 Grok Bot：X Mentor 盯 90 天发帖计划（早 8 点简报、晚 9 点复盘、周日系统回顾和连续打卡）；Ops CEO 严抓执行；Study Coach 负责工科预科课业。当天导师把他关于核函数和基尔霍夫定律的笔记整理成研究日志，他读后批准。原则：机器人少、一个一事、未经他同意不外发。

What it does
The author built 3 Grok Bots with one job each: X Mentor holds him to a 90-day posting plan (8am brief, 9pm check-in, Sunday review, streak tracker); Ops CEO enforces execution; Study Coach runs his prep-class engineering work. That day the mentor turned his notes on kernels and Kirchhoff's law into a research log, which he read and approved. Rule: few bots, one job each, nothing goes out without him.
