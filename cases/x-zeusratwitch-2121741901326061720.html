# 警示：Grok Bot 例程一天推 80 条回复，账号曝光被清零

Source: https://x.com/ZeusRaTwitch/status/2121741901326061720

Author: ZeusRaTwitch

作者说曾让 Grok Bot 规划「不被判定为垃圾信息的最多回复数」，结果它设了一个例程，每天给约 80 条一键回复链接，每批 10 条，没有强制间隔。11 月 14 日他集中发出：约上午 9:05 一分钟内约 18 条、10:13 约 8 条、17:53 约 15 条（美东时间）。之后所有回复曝光为 0，常规帖子浏览量也降到 2–3。作者认为助手应内置更低的默认量、强制间隔，并在计划接近平台垃圾信息模式时明确叫停。

What it does
The author asked for the maximum number of X replies he could post without being flagged. Grok Bot set up a routine delivering about 80 one-tap reply links a day in batches of 10, with no forced spacing. On Nov 14 he posted them in bursts: about 18 in a minute around 9:05 AM ET, 8 around 10:13 AM and 15 around 5:53 PM. Afterward every reply showed 0 impressions and his regular posts fell to 2–3 views. He argues the assistant should default to lower volume, enforce spacing and stop plans that match the platform's spam patterns.
