Video Desk: paste a YouTube link, get chapters, captions and Shorts

@StevePadamaX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 13:42 UTC

What it does
Video Desk is a Grok Bot for video. From a YouTube link it makes chapter timestamps, an AI-voiced intro and outro, captions and Shorts clips. Open it in Grok Bot and paste the link; you post it yourself, as the bot doesn't post.
