Steps / points from the source
  1. You set the bar (gold sample + score sheet).
  2. CoS routes the work.
  3. Worker produces one artifact against a frozen skill.
  4. Auditor scores yes/no against the checklist until every row is yes.
  5. Repeat on a second real walk. Auditor all-yes twice before we call the skill mature.
