AbleLatch, a free Grok Bot template for people with disabilities and limited input

@CaliLumberJackX ↗Posted 2026-10-31Added 2026-10-31 22:36 UTC♥ 4

What it does
The author released AbleLatch, a free Grok Bot template with no paid tiers. Every screen is a short number menu where 8 is always Emergency and 0 is always Stop. It works with eye-gaze, switch, voice, sip-and-puff, AAC apps, BCI typing or a caregiver answering, in any language. It covers medication reminders, rides, groceries, home and VA help, and nothing is sent or bought without a yes.
