Keep Grok Bot from acting before you have looked

@kv1nsiiiX ↗Posted 2026-10-22Added 2026-10-22 22:41 UTC♥ 19

What it does
The author warns that with loose rules Grok Bot reacts to new messages and calendar shifts by replying and booking on its own. His rules: read first and draft only for real changes, stop empty checks after one look, surface only items that need your tap, send or book nothing without you, and note small shifts instead of making tasks.
