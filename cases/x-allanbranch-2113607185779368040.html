A Grok Bot that finds the why behind leases and policies

@allanbranchX ↗Posted 2026-10-23Added 2026-10-23 12:39 UTC

What it does
Leases, insurance policies and HR docs record what was agreed but not why; the reasons sit in old emails and meetings. The author built a Grok Bot that pulls those reasons out and logs them next to the documents so the team doesn’t have to guess.
