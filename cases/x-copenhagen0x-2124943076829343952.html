# 给 Grok Bot 每个工具设权限：只读放行，不可撤回的要审批

Source: https://x.com/Copenhagen0x/status/2124943076829343952

Author: Copenhagen0x

作者结合 Walmart 允许智能体结账并设消费上限，介绍 Grok Bot 可为每个连接应用的工具设四档：始终允许、自动审核、需要批准、禁止。建议：读取放始终允许，草稿放自动审核，发邮件、删除、付款放需要批准，用不到的禁止；并在指令中写明「别买，先给我看总价」。附黑五礼物提示词。

What it does
Citing Walmart's new agent checkout with spending caps, the author explains Grok Bot's four per-tool settings for connected apps: Always allow, Auto-review, Needs approval and Blocked. His sorting: reading on Always allow, drafts on Auto-review, emailing customers, deleting and paying on Needs approval, unneeded tools Blocked. He also writes limits into the ask ("Don't buy. Show me the total first") and shares a Black Friday gift prompt.
