Steps / points from the source
  1. Type "menu" to see everything
  2. Type "uplift" for quotes: AI jokes, Monty Python, philosophers, Star Wars, Spaceballs, or Bible verses (can't pick? say "I want it all")
  3. Type "news" for recent rocket, Tesla, or Boring news
  4. Type "credits" to thank the KDE devs 💙
  5. Or type a drink like "earl grey", "raptor roast", or "ludicrous moka" to start a timer
  6. Type "start breaks" for stretch and water reminders, brought to you by the lovable Marvin from HHGTTG 🤖
  7. Busy? Type "skip", "postpone", or "suspend"
  8. bot-rules.md has the bot's full instructions (its "source").
