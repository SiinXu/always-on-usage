Editing a launch video with a dot, it removed too much

@gheedbizri21X ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 23:47 UTC

What it does
The author was making an app launch video with his dot. The video had gold text labels and a small character; he asked it to remove labels like "starting piece" but keep the reactions, and it removed the character too. After he clarified he meant the labels, it restored the character. He wishes it would check before removing something that might matter.
