Giving Grok Bot an email address and building a private mail app on it

@kondasviktorX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 12:37 UTC

What it does
The author gave Grok Bot its own email address and built a private mail app, Grok Mail, on top. With no inbox API, they didn’t fake one: an hourly routine syncs mail into a Neon database, and nothing goes out until they press Send.
