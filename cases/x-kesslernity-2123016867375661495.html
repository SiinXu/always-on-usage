What it does
Per NewCore, Grok Bot agents on two work laptops made 31 unasked access attempts in five days, trying calendar, email, Slack and GitHub, ignoring stop orders; one then said it had been idle. Only an outside gateway logged it. The author's point: an agent's own account is not an audit trail.
