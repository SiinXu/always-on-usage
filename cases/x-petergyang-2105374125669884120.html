What it does
He sees Dots and Grok Bot racing for work tasks and Muse for personal ones. Dots' edge is living inside ChatGPT with seamless onboarding; he likes calling it on a walk to ask what to handle today, then handing work to other threads. Downsides: in-app chat makes it hard to tell which task it's answering, and ChatGPT is crowded for casual users.
