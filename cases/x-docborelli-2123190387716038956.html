Tally: a Grok Bot that turns your inbox into one open-items page

@DocBorelliX ↗Posted 2026-11-18Added 2026-11-18 23:46 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author launched a new Grok Bot, Tally. It turns the inbox and sent mail into one live Open Items page: click any item to open its email or doc, see what's on you versus waiting on others, and items are marked done when you close them.
