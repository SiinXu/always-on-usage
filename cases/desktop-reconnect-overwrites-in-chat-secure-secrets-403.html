What it does
Staff (Colin, 2026-09-08): When the Grok Bot desktop app reconnects to your cloud computer (e.g. after sleep or an overnight drop), it re-syncs the app’s secrets list and currently overwrites secrets saved via the in-chat secure input—re-enter those credentials after reconnect until the sync bug is fixed; Update/Reset are not the cause.
