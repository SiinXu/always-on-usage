Mac 0.43: lost computer after password change — sign out/in, don’t Reset

RongleCat/awesome-grok-bot (GitHub) ↗

Title and summary are in the original language, not translated.

What it does
Staff (deanrie, 2026-09-06): On 0.43.0 Mac, a Cursor password change/reset (or sign-out elsewhere) can invalidate the desktop session so the app keeps the old token and shows “Couldn’t Reach Grok Bot’s Computer,” with Update/Recover/Reset also failing—computer and Bots are fine; sign out and back in on desktop (Quit from menu bar if Sign out is missing), don’t Reset.
