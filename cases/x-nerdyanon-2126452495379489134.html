# 用 Grok Bot 做河流洪水监测机器人

Source: https://x.com/Nerdy_Anon/status/2126452495379489134

Author: Nerdy_Anon

作者在 Grok Bot 上搭了一个 River Flood Watch：选定 NOAA 河流水位站及上游站点，每天收到带幻灯片的简短预报和带不确定区间的洪峰估计；平时保持安静，只在河流达到洪水位或 NWS 发布洪水产品时提醒。数据只用 NOAA/NWS 和 USGS 官方来源。

What it does
The author built a River Flood Watch bot on Grok Bot: pick a NOAA river gauge plus the upstream ones, get a short daily forecast with slides and a crest estimate with an uncertainty range, and stay quiet unless the river hits flood stage or the NWS issues a flood product. It uses only official NOAA/NWS and USGS data.
