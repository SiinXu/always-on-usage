Grok Bot is replaceable: the model matters more than the platform

@arnoldgamboaX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 23:41 UTC

What it does
The author runs much of his day on Grok Bot but is moving bots to OpenBot one by one, testing both side by side daily. So far his routine carries over. He will miss Grok Bot’s big team and steady improvements, but finds the bigger difference is the model: one he pays for on his own subscription can be used freely without fear of losing his routines.
