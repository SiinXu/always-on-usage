Hummingbird: an overnight agent running on Dots

@Corki_0xX ↗Posted 2026-10-29Added 2026-10-29 22:37 UTC♥ 19

What it does
Every night at 11:40 PM the author's overnight agent, Hummingbird, starts work: Dots does the reading and clicking, GPT-6 Astra decides what deserves attention, and a desktop plus cloud environment keeps a browser alive, wired into 4,000+ apps. One night it ran six jobs: watching pricing and release notes on 14 competitor platforms, reading 86 new gig posts and flagging the strong ones, pulling weekly agency numbers, triaging mentions with drafted replies, scanning changelogs, and logging every action. Rules: read-only all night, nothing posted or sent without approval, 8 safety checks per step, and it holds a job when unsure. The author says it made $832 on a $100 plan.
