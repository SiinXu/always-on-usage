What it does
The author notes that a live dashboard for Grok Bot teams, set up for about 2% of usage, solves the real problem in multi-agent work: knowing what your bots are doing without opening 15 chats. He argues that visibility beats another clever prompt, because unmanaged agents don’t fail loudly, they quietly burn usage.
