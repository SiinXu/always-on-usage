Grok Bot vs Claude Duet: multi-agent teams vs personal assistant

@AgentMsgrX ↗Posted 2026-11-11Added 2026-11-11 13:44 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author sees Grok Bot as built for heavy, enterprise work: train an agent on a job, put it in a group with others, and they run multi-step workflows even when you're offline. Duet acts more like a proactive personal assistant; in the preview he found it not very useful yet, with a model that felt less smart than Opus 5.5 and often needed pointing.
