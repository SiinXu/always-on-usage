# 每周日晚让 Grok Bot 检查下周日程冲突

Source: https://x.com/theaigrandpa/status/2121734379445649745

Author: theaigrandpa

作者建议把日历连接到 Grok Bot，并给它一句指令：「每周日晚上 7 点读取我这一周的日程，标出冲突和排得太满的日子，然后给我发一份简短计划。」用来避免像足球训练和看牙医撞在同一个周二这类情况。

What it does
The author suggests connecting your calendar to Grok Bot and telling it: "Every Sunday at 7 PM, read my week. Flag clashes and packed days, then send me a short plan." The aim is to catch things like soccer practice and the dentist landing on the same Tuesday.
