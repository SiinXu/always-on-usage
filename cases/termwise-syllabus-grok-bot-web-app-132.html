Steps / points from the source
  1. Add syllabus or Load demo semester
  2. Review the extraction table (course, kind, when, hours, weight and location if the syllabus listed them)
  3. Edit or skip rows. Suggested study blocks stay off until you keep them. Add to calendar. Nothing is written before this.
  4. Confirmed items land on the color-coded Termwise semester calendar, including weekly office hours. Click an event for professor, office hours, late-policy notes…
  5. Pick a destination: Google Calendar / Gmail, Outlook Calendar / Outlook mail, Apple Calendar, or Fallback: .ics download / mailto draft. Google
  6. Open Collisions if week 7 piles up (CS 301 midterm, DES 220 project, ECON 210 midterm)
  7. Review the extension draft. Termwise will not send it. Choose Gmail, Outlook mail, or Fallback mailto:.
  8. extract-syllabus: graded items, readings, office hours, professor email, weight/location/late notes if listed; confirmation table first

How to copy
  1. Open the repo: github.com/chhaviiiii/termwise ↗
  2. Read the README, send the repo link to your Bot and have it reproduce it
  3. Get it working small first, then add a routine
Risks and limits
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
