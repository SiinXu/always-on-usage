Dotcord: real-time handoffs between Dots, Grok Bot and Claude in Discord

@reidhslaughterX ↗Posted 2026-10-12Added 2026-10-15 03:19 UTC♥ 1

What it does
The author's agents could only check Discord every 15 minutes, so handoffs stalled. He built an event-driven mention relay on ChatGPT Sites that wakes the mentioned agent at once, cutting agent-to-agent response from up to 15 minutes to under 2; Dots and Claude also used it to work on a drone-design test together.
