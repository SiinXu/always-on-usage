Emptying my head into OpenAI Dot before and after work

@yousefrolX ↗Posted 2026-10-31Added 2026-10-31 22:36 UTC♥ 55

What it does
Before starting and after finishing work (usually before bed), the author talks to OpenAI Dot to unload their thoughts. The next day it explains their ideas and intent back, follows their projects, runs security agents on them and keeps watching all day. It also collects their prompts, discusses any new pattern and adds it to their skills so they can mention a skill instead of rewriting. Weekend schedules, a study plan and a summary of what followers say go to it too.
