Skills in Grok Bot: save a working process once

@z0renXX ↗Posted 2026-11-12Added 2026-11-12 13:41 UTC♥ 2

What it does
Once a task works, save the whole process as a skill that keeps the steps, rules, output format and approval points; any of your bots can then call it by name. You can also teach a skill by doing the task once on the bot's computer, and it records it as something reusable.
