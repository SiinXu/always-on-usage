A one-hour morning dashboard for many Grok Bot bots

@TonyChanBuildsX ↗Posted 2026-10-29Added 2026-10-29 12:46 UTC♥ 2

What it does
With too many bots and too much jumping between chats, the author built one dashboard in about an hour that shows what every bot is working on and what needs them. They share the prompt for the main Grok Bot: a page to check each morning with the 5 numbers that matter most this week, top 3 priorities, decisions waiting on them and today's calendar, pulled from other bots and tools, refreshed before they wake and readable in 1 minute. They credit @AlexFinn for the idea.

Prompt
Build me one dashboard page I can check every morning. Show the 5 numbers that matter most to my goals this week, my top 3 priorities, a list of decisions you're waiting on me for, and today's calendar. Pull the info from my other bots and tools.
