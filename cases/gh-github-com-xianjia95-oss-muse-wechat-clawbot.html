muse-wechat-clawbot: connecting Meta Muse to WeChat ClawBot

xianjia95-ossGitHub ↗Posted 2026-10-21Added 2026-10-21 12:42 UTC

What it does
An open-source lightweight bridge between Meta Muse and WeChat ClawBot: text in and out, returning generated images and videos, task routing, send de-duplication and timing logs. Built on Tencent's iLink protocol in Node.js, MIT licensed.
