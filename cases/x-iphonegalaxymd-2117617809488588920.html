Overnight AI profits from an old phone? Check the risks first

@iphonegalaxymdX ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC

What it does
The author notes that posts about an old phone and an agent making money overnight rarely show proof. Plausible: spare phones as always-on agent hardware and assistants like Grok Bot running scheduled routines. Not proven: overnight trading profits. The risk is that an old phone is already logged in to apps, so anything the agent can tap, it can spend. Advice: use a spare phone, sign in only to needed apps, keep logins, 2FA and payments behind human approval, and start with boring jobs.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • Uses a browser or website login: the costliest kind of step, so prefer a connector where one exists
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
