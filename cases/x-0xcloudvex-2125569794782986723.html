# 可以直接交给 Grok Bot 的 10 件活和任务公式

Source: https://x.com/0xcloudvex/status/2125569794782986723

Author: 0xcloudvex

What it does
The author lists 10 starter jobs, such as sorting the inbox each morning and drafting replies, flagging subscriptions unused for 30 days, watching a price, turning a video into X posts and sending last week's numbers every Monday at 8 AM. The formula: do [job] for [who] using [files/apps], done when [result], never [limit], show me before you send. It should always ask before messaging as you, spending, posting publicly, deleting, or booking and cancelling.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
