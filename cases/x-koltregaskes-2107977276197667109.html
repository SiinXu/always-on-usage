What it does
The author explains Dots custom rules: on the web they sit under Personalisation → Permissions; each rule reads “When my Dot wants to… my Dot should…”, and you can ask the Dot to draft rules and confirm with a button. He quotes an example rule for a Linear workspace (see source text below).
