Muse and a Dot are replacing the apps he used to open

@_simonsmithX ↗Posted 2026-11-10Added 2026-11-10 13:42 UTC♥ 3

What it does
The author says personal agents are displacing many apps. Muse triages his email every morning, so he rarely opens Gmail (mainly to create filters, which Muse can't do in the app yet). It built a "Space" combining his Google and iCloud calendars and Todoist tasks, plus a news aggregator that summarizes and alerts on key sources. At work, his Dot is his main interface to Google Workspace: he fires tasks at it from Slack and gets deliverables back without opening the apps.
