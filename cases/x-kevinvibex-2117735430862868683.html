Grok Bot read a year of bank statements and cut 7 subscriptions

@Kevin_VibeXX ↗Posted 2026-11-03Added 2026-11-03 23:44 UTC♥ 15

What it does
The author exported 12 months of statements (1,904 transactions) and had Grok Bot tag them: $3,941 a month, 38% housing, 19% food. Based on actual usage it cancelled 7 idle or double-charged subscriptions ($172.95 a month) and kept the ones in use, then drafted emails to negotiate internet, phone and car insurance bills. The author says the total saving is $2,819 a year.
