Choosing between GrokBot, Claude Max and ChatGPT Pro, he picked ChatGPT Pro

@DedeChandraNX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 23:42 UTC

What it does
The author chose ChatGPT Pro at $100. His reasons: Dots, the always-on agent, is still exploratory and similar to GrokBot but doesn't seem to support multiple bots yet; chatting with Dots doesn't eat ChatGPT/Codex weekly usage; there's no 5-hour limit like Claude Max; $100 a month in API credits; frequent usage resets; the subscription works in Hermes Agent; and GPT-6.1 Sol is enough for him, though he admits Opus 5.5 is very good.
