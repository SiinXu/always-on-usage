Steps / points from the source
  1. Daily Webhook Snapshot — On every scan, POSTs full ticker JSON (gates, technical indicators, asset archetype, earnings status, tranche levels) to Grok Bot web…
  2. GitHub Issue on Accumulation Trigger — Automatically opens one GitHub Issue per ticker only when technical confluence passes (signal=ENTRY, confluence=true
  3. IBM (asset_type: equity) — Core enterprise AI/hybrid cloud dividend compounder. Subject to single-name earnings blackouts.
  4. QTUM (asset_type: etf) — Defiance Quantum ETF (~70 equal-weighted tech/quantum holdings). Earnings blackout marked N/A.
  5. AIPO (asset_type: etf) — Defiance AI & Power Infrastructure ETF (grid, nuclear, data center utilities). Surfaces indicator maturity status.
  6. Philosophy: Standard institutional accumulation into minor consolidations during established uptrends.
  7. Trend Requirement: 50 SMA is upward-sloping (SMA50[t] > SMA50[t - 10]) and close is within −1.5% to +3.0% of the 50 SMA.
  8. Pullback Requirement: Daily RSI 14 pulled back into the 35 to 48 consolidation zone.
