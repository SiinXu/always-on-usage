A call-screening AI built with Grok Bot templates

@xenappblogX ↗Posted 2026-10-28Added 2026-10-28 22:52 UTC

What it does
Using Grok Bot's Vibe Coder and CTO templates, the author set up a personal AI that screens incoming calls: spam and robocalls get hung up on, real callers leave a message, and a note in the Grok Bot app says who called and why. Built on Twilio and Cloudflare Workers with voice from the Grok API; the first real call took 56 seconds and cost under 10 cents.
