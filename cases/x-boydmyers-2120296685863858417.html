What it does
A former home-sales agent had his chief-of-staff bot gather the material and spin up a new bot to own the site. The first draft was bad; after a few iterations it was good enough, and with the domain registrar already connected the bots bought a domain and pushed Mortgage Unpacked live. It teaches buyers to read their situation like an underwriter and pulls rates dynamically, all in under 90 minutes.
