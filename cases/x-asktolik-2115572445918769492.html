What it does
The author automates video creation with Grok Bot, but some videos had editing mistakes or awkward timing. Two changes fixed it: connecting Grok Bot to API Hub, a platform his team built for creating and editing videos, and giving it a Video QA skill that reviews every video before publishing, with nothing going out unless it passes.
