Building a 14-department org out of Grok Bot

@razeden0X ↗Posted 2026-10-31Added 2026-10-31 22:36 UTC♥ 2

What it does
Following an article, the author spent two evenings building a hierarchy: Grok Bot as the boss, 14 lead bots running departments and about 200 agents doing the work, with output landing in /workspace/drafts for approval. Steps: a 10-minute voice memo that Grok Bot turned into 14 job cards, 5 auto-review rules instead of approvals, and starting with 1 bot and adding 1 a day so two bots never fight over one job. They report that in week one the inbox handled 2,393 of 2,431 emails and QA ran 126 tests with 0 fails, for about $202 a month. Anything involving money or a signature waits for their yes.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
