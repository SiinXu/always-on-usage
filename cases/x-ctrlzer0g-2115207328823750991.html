# Grok Bot 夜里写了 63 封邮件草稿，一封没发

Source: https://x.com/ctrlzer0g/status/2115207328823750991

Author: ctrlzer0g

作者睡前只给一条规则：把活干完，但没我同意什么都不发。早上 7 点得到 63 封草稿（最久未回的优先）、3 个附理由的紧急线程、2 个建议取消的会议、1 封金额留空的发票回复，发出 0 封。喝咖啡时 20 分钟批准 41 封、重写 9 封、删掉其余，以前要花两小时。用的是 Chief of Staff 任务模板，从 Slack、邮箱、日历和会议记录取材。

What it does
The author's only rule before bed: do the work, send nothing without my yes. At 7am there were 63 drafts (oldest unanswered first), 3 urgent threads with reasons, 2 meetings to drop, 1 invoice reply with the amount left blank, and 0 sent. Over coffee they approved 41 in 20 minutes, rewrote 9 and deleted the rest, versus two hours before. It used the chief of staff job, which pulls from Slack, email, calendar and meeting notes.
