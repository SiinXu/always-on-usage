Grok Bot Import Memory: switch assistants without starting over

@kiamlordX ↗Posted 2026-11-01Added 2026-11-01 13:46 UTC

What it does
Steps: Settings, General, Start Import; copy the prompt into your other assistant, paste its answer back and click Add to Memory, which goes to your primary bot. The docs say logins, cards and contact info belong in Vault, so the author cuts them before pasting, then asks 'What do you remember about me?' to check.
