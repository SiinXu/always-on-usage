What it does
The author wrote briefs for 10 marketing roles and connected the campaign: Plan (CMO, audience research, offer), Create (copy, creative, SEO), Distribute (social, paid growth, lifecycle email) and Learn (analytics and CRO). One brief feeds every channel, each bot returns a named artifact, and analytics routes the next test back to the makers: 10 roles, 26 handoffs. The author notes the blueprint needs account and tool setup, and service fees are separate.

Risks and limits
  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Several bots in parallel: usage adds up per bot, so merge bots with similar jobs first
