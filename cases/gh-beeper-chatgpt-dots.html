Steps / points from the source
  1. Text messages, including incoming Markdown formatting.
  2. Images, files and videos in both directions, with a bridge-local 20 MiB limit.
  3. Task cards whose titles and status update in place.
  4. Incoming Dot emoji reactions, typing indicators and read receipts.
  5. Separate Dot and account avatars, account profile refresh and session renewal.
  6. Recovery of messages received after linking, including across restarts.
