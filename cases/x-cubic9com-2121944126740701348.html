Wiring a TT-motor four-wheel car from a diagram worked out with Meta Muse

@cubic9comX ↗Posted 2026-11-15Added 2026-11-15 13:38 UTC

What it does
The author settled on a wiring diagram for a TT-motor four-wheel car by talking it through with Meta Muse, then wired it to match, using a Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C5 and a TB6612FNG. A capacitor against voltage drop at motor start will be added later; it was the author's first time using polyurethane-coated copper wire.
