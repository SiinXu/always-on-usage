Guardrails that make Grok Bot do less

@perpycodeX ↗Posted 2026-11-09Added 2026-11-09 13:41 UTC

What it does
The author argues for asking Grok Bot to do less and lists guardrails: one job per bot; email and Slack stay as draft cards until you hit send; Vault Payments still needs your approval for every purchase; set Auto-review to Always Ask for anything you can't undo; new routines stay off until you turn them on; share Pages and Library on purpose, not by default.

Risks and limits
