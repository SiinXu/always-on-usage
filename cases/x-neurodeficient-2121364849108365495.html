What it does
The author notes Grok Bot started joining meetings and on Mac can use Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location and Contacts. He suggests starting with one small job like a Reminders cleanup or a Calendar check, and keeping approval on anything that sends a message or email so it drafts and you tap yes.
