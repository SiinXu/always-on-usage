How the three always-on agents differ: Grok Bot is a team, Dots and Muse work solo

@tifodenX ↗Posted 2026-10-15Added 2026-10-16 11:12 UTC

What it does
The author compares three always-on agents. Grok Bot was designed from the start as a team of bots with roles that message each other and learn from demonstrations; Dots runs inside ChatGPT and so far centres on one main Dot; Muse focuses on personal life and small-business errands. For one-off requests they feel similar; the gap shows when you assign lasting roles.
