What it does

Grok Bot scanned the calendars for things that needed a reservation, worked out the best time to book, and completed the booking on the website — while the author talked to it in mixed Chinese and English

Prompt

The post does not include a verbatim prompt; the steps below are this site's reading of the post.

Steps to try

Compiled by this site from the post, not the author's words.

  1. Connect your calendar
  2. Instruction: find upcoming plans that need a reservation but don't have one
  3. Let the Bot book in the browser and confirm before any payment
  4. Run it weekly

Risks and limits

  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Uses a browser or website login: the costliest kind of step, so prefer a connector where one exists
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
