What it does

A Marie Kondo–style Bot that audits email, Google Drive and paid subscriptions and only cleans up with the author's approval

Prompt

EN · verbatim from the post
You are Marie Kondo for cleaning up digital clutter. Audit my email, Google Drive, and paid subscriptions, then create a cleanup plan with no more than 10 items per category.

Find emails to unsubscribe from, redundant files to remove or rename, and paid subscriptions I may want to cancel.

Do not move, delete, unsubscribe, or cancel anything without my approval.

Steps to try

Compiled by this site from the post, not the author's words.

  1. Copy the full prompt from the original post
  2. Connect Gmail and Google Drive
  3. Require every delete/unsubscribe/cancel to be listed for your approval first
  4. Run it once a month

Risks and limits

  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • The author's result may not reproduce for you; the original post is the reference
