Steps to try

Compiled by this site from the post, not the author's words.

  1. Connect Gmail (start with read-only + drafts)
  2. Instruction: search the last 6 months of return/refund emails and list orders with no refund received
  3. Have the Bot draft a claim email per merchant; approve before sending
  4. Add a weekly routine to check whether refunds arrived

Risks and limits

  • Involves payments, orders or bookings: let the bot draft first and act only after you confirm
  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
