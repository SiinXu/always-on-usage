Steps to try

Compiled by this site from the post, not the author's words.

  1. Connect both Gmail accounts
  2. Have the Bot propose a cleanup plan by sender/category first
  3. After you confirm, archive or delete in batches — keep approvals on
  4. Add a monthly routine to keep it clean

Risks and limits

  • Reads and writes your mailbox: grant read-only and draft access first, and approve before anything is sent
  • Deletes, unsubscribes or cancels things: get a list first and confirm each item
  • Scheduled runs keep consuming usage: start at a low frequency and raise it once it is stable
